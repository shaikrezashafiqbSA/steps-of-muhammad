## Duʿāʾ al-Iftitāḥ — The Opening Supplication of Prayer

### 📜 The Hadith
This supplication is recited silently after the opening Takbīr (Takbīrat al-Iḥrām), before Sūrah al-Fātiḥah, in the first rakʿah of prayer. In the Shāfiʿī school of jurisprudence, it is a recommended Sunnah (Sunnah Hayʾah).

The commonly recited formulation combines two prophetic narrations. Its first section comes from the narration of ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhumā), who reported that while they were praying with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, a man said: "Allāhu akbaru kabīrā, wal-ḥamdu lillāhi kathīrā, wa subḥānallāhi bukratan wa aṣīlā." The Messenger of Allah ﷺ asked who said those words, and then remarked: "I was amazed at them; the gates of heaven were opened for them."

Its second section comes from the narration of ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭālib (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu), who reported that when the Messenger of Allah ﷺ stood up for prayer, he would say: "Wajjahtu wajhiya lilladhī faṭaras-samāwāti wal-arḍa ḥanīfan musliman wa mā ana minal-mushrikīn… wa ana minal-muslimīn."
