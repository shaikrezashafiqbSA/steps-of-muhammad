# Duʿāʾ al-Iftitāḥ — The Opening Supplication of the Prayer

## 📜 The Hadith

This supplication is recited silently after the opening Takbīr (*Takbīratul-Iḥrām*), before Sūrah al-Fātiḥah, in the first rakʿah. In the Shāfiʿī school it is a *Sunnah Hayʾah* — recommended, and omitting it neither invalidates the prayer nor requires *sujūd sahw*.

The commonly recited Shāfiʿī form joins two prophetic narrations. Its first half comes from the report of Ibn ʿUmar (r.a.), who described a man praying behind the Prophet ﷺ who said, *"Allāhu akbaru kabīrā, wal-ḥamdu lillāhi kathīrā, wa subḥānallāhi bukratan wa aṣīlā,"* upon which the Prophet ﷺ remarked that the gates of heaven were opened for it. Its second half comes from ʿAlī (r.a.), who reported that when the Messenger of Allah ﷺ began his prayer he would say, *"Wajjahtu wajhiya lilladhī faṭaras-samāwāti wal-arḍ… wa ana minal-muslimīn."*

The Text (المَتْن)
[[ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ كَبِيرًا وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ كَثِيرًا وَسُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ بُكْرَةً وَأَصِيلًا | Allah is Most Great; abundant praise be to Allah; and glory be to Allah, morning and evening | Allāhu akbaru kabīrā, wal-ḥamdu lillāhi kathīrā, wa subḥānallāhi bukratan wa aṣīlā ]]

[[ وَجَّهْتُ وَجْهِيَ لِلَّذِي فَطَرَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ حَنِيفًا مُسْلِمًا وَمَا أَنَا مِنَ الْمُشْرِكِينَ | I have turned my face to the One who originated the heavens and the earth, inclining to truth, in submission, and I am not among those who associate partners with Allah | wajjahtu wajhiya lilladhī faṭaras-samāwāti wal-arḍa ḥanīfan musliman wa mā ana minal-mushrikīn ]]

[[ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحْيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ وَبِذَلِكَ أُمِرْتُ وَأَنَا مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ | Indeed my prayer, my sacrifice, my living and my dying are for Allah, Lord of the worlds; He has no partner; with that I have been commanded, and I am among those who submit | inna ṣalātī wa nusukī wa maḥyāya wa mamātī lillāhi rabbil-ʿālamīn, lā sharīka lahu, wa bidhālika umirtu wa ana minal-muslimīn ]]

Textual note
[[ حَنِيفًا | inclining to truth — in Qurʾān 6:79 and Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 771 this reads "ḥanīfan" alone; "musliman" is an addition in the common Shāfiʿī formula | ḥanīfan ]]

## 🔤 Word-by-Word

[[ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ كَبِيرًا | Allah is Most Great | Allāhu akbaru kabīrā ]]

[[ وَسُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ بُكْرَةً وَأَصِيلًا | and glory be to Allah, morning and evening | wa subḥānallāhi bukratan wa aṣīlā ]]

[[ وَجَّهْتُ وَجْهِيَ | I have turned my face | wajjahtu wajhiya ]]

[[ حَنِيفًا مُسْلِمًا | inclining to truth, in submission | ḥanīfan musliman ]]

[[ وَمَحْيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي | and my living and my dying | wa maḥyāya wa mamātī ]]

[[ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ | are for Allah, Lord of the worlds | lillāhi rabbil-ʿālamīn ]]

[[ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ | He has no partner | lā sharīka lahu ]]

[[ وَبِذَلِكَ أُمِرْتُ | and with that I have been commanded | wa bidhālika umirtu ]]

[[ وَأَنَا مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ | and I am among those who submit | wa ana minal-muslimīn ]]
