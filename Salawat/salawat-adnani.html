صَلَّى عَلَيْكَ اللَّهُ يَا عَدْنَانِي

The Prophetic Praise of ‘Abdul Muṭṭalib – Historical poem & Selawat chorus

📖 Historical Context

When the Prophet’s grandfather, ‘Abdul Muṭṭalib, carried the newborn Muhammad (ﷺ) into the Ka‘bah, he stood in prayer to thank Allah and recited these verses of praise and protection. This poem is documented in Dalā’il al-Nubuwwah by Imām al-Bayhaqī, Sīrah of Ibn Isḥāq, and Ṭabaqāt al-Kubrā by Ibn Sa‘d. The modern refrain “Şallā ‘alayka Allāhu yā ‘Adnānī” was added in contemporary gatherings as a collective selawat.

📜 The Narration Chain & Historical Report

‘Abdullāh ibn ‘Abbās transmitted from ‘Abdul Muṭṭalib:
"When the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) was born, his grandfather ‘Abdul Muṭṭalib took him, entered the Ka‘bah, stood in supplication, and praised Allah with these verses, seeking protection for him from every evil."
The Arabic Narration (Biographical report)
لَمَّا when lammā
وُلِدَ was born wulida
رَسُولُ messenger of rasūlu
ٱللَّهِ allah allāhi
ﷺ peace be upon him ṣallallāhu ‘alayhi wasallam
أَخَذَهُ took him akhadhahū
جَدُّهُ his grandfather jadduhū
عَبْدُ abdul ‘abdu
ٱلْمُطَّلِبِ al-muttalib al-muṭṭalib
فَدَخَلَ and entered fadakhal
ٱلْكَعْبَةَ the ka‘bah al-ka‘bah
فَقَامَ then stood faqāma
يَدْعُو supplicating yad‘ū
وَيَقُولُ and saying wayaqūlu
"When the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) was born, his grandfather ‘Abdul Muṭṭalib took him, entered the Ka‘bah, stood in supplication and said:"
‘Abdullāh ibn ‘Abbās (d. 68 AH / 687 CE)

Full name: ‘Abdullāh ibn ‘Abbās ibn ‘Abdil-Muṭṭalib al-Hāshimī. Cousin of the Prophet ﷺ, renowned as Ḥabr al-Ummah (the scholar of this nation).

He transmitted countless reports about the birth of the Prophet and the events surrounding it, preserving the poem of his grandfather ‘Abdul Muṭṭalib.

Role here: The primary chain through which this poem reached later historians like Ibn Isḥāq and al-Bayhaqī.

📜 Part 2 · The Historical Poem of ‘Abdul Muṭṭalib
ٱلْحَمْدُ all praise al-ḥamdu
لِلَّهِ to allah lillāhi
ٱلَّذِي who alladhī
أَعْطَانِي granted me a‘ṭānī
هَذَا this hādhā
ٱلْغُلَامَ the boy al-ghulām
ٱلطَّيِّبَ the pure aṭ-ṭayyiba
ٱلْأَرْدَانِ immaculate (clean sleeves) al-ardānī
"Praise be to Allah Who has granted me this boy, who is pure and immaculate in character."
Ṣaḥīḥ – Recorded by al-Bayhaqī Dalā’il al-Nubuwwah

سَادَ has excelled sāda
فِي in fī
ٱلْمَهْدِ the cradle al-mahdi
عَلَى over ‘alā
ٱلْغِلْمَانِ the youths al-ghilmān

"He has already excelled over all other youths while still in his cradle; I place him under the protection of the House (the Ka‘bah) which possesses mighty pillars."

✅ Source & Authenticity

The primary historical poem of ‘Abdul Muṭṭalib is authentically transmitted in Dalā’il al-Nubuwwah of Imām al-Bayhaqī (Ṣaḥīḥ), Sīrat Ibn Isḥāq, and Kitāb al-Ṭabaqāt al-Kubrā of Ibn Sa‘d. The modern selawat refrain, though not part of the original historical text, is widely used by scholars as a legitimate form of ṣalawāt upon the Prophet ﷺ, which is encouraged in Qur’an (33:56) and ḥadīth.
