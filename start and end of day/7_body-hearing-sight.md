## Allāhumma ʿĀfinī Fī Badanī — Supplications for Vital Well-being, Protection, and Relief from Distress

### 📜 The Hadith
ʿAbdur-Raḥmān ibn Abī Bakrah reported that he told his father: 
"O my father! I hear you supplicating every morning <dua below> You repeat them three times in the morning and three times in the evening."

He replied: *"I heard the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) using these words as a supplication and I love to follow his practice."*

*"You repeat them three times in the morning and three times in the evening, and use them as a supplication. I love to follow his practice."*
