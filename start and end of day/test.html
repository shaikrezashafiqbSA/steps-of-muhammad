اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَ سَلِّمْ عَلَى نَبِيِّنَا مُحَمَّد

Morning & Evening Salawat – Seeking the Prophet’s Intercession

📖 Context of the Invocation

This du'ā is recited ten times in the morning and ten times in the evening. It invokes Allah’s blessings and peace upon Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) and is a means to secure his intercession on the Day of Resurrection. The instruction comes from a reliable narration recorded by Imam al-Ṭabarānī.

The Narration Chain & Context (Part 1)

The Prophet (ﷺ) said:
"Who recites blessings upon me ten times in the morning and ten times in the evening will obtain my intercession on the Day of Resurrection."
The Arabic Narration (Word-for-Word)
مَنْ whoever man
صَلَّى recites blessings ṣallā
عَلَيَّ upon me ʿalayya
عَشْرَ ten ʿashra
مَرَّاتٍ times marrātin
فِي in fī
الغَدَاةِ the morning al-ghadāti
وَعَشْرًا and ten wa ʿashran
فِي in fī
المَسَاءِ the evening al-masā'i
حَصَلَتْ will obtain ḥaṣalat
لَهُ for him lahū
شَفَاعَتِي my intercession shafāʿatī
يَوْمَ on the day yawma
القِيَامَةِ of resurrection al-qiyāmah
"Who recites blessings upon me ten times in the morning and ten times in the evening will obtain my intercession on the Day of Resurrection."
Part 1 · The Morning & Evening Salawāt
اللَّهُمَّ o allah allāhumma
صَلِّ bless ṣalli
وَ and wa
سَلِّمْ grant peace sallim
عَلَى upon ʿalā
نَبِيِّنَا our prophet nabiyyinā
مُحَمَّدٍ muhammad muḥammad
O Allah, we ask for your peace and blessings upon our Prophet Muhammad.
Jayyid (Reliable) At-Ṭabarānī, two chains; one is reliable (Jayyid). See Haythami’s Majma‘uz-Zawā’id 10/120, Al-Albānī’s Ṣaḥīḥ at-Targhīb wat-Tarhīb 1/273.
📿 Practice Note

Recite ten times in the morning and ten times in the evening. The morning time begins after Fajr ṣalāh until sunrise, or until ḍuḥā. Evening begins after ‘Aṣr until sunset. Consistency brings the promised intercession.

The Narration Chain & Source Analysis (Part 2)

Imam At-Ṭabarānī & Al-Haythamī transmitted:
"At-Ṭabarānī reported this Ḥadīth together with two chains of transmission. One of them is reliable (Jayyid). Al-Haythamī included it in Majma‘uz-Zawā’id and declared one chain sound. Shaykh Al-Albānī graded it Ḥasan in Ṣaḥīḥ at-Targhīb."
Transmission word‑by‑word (summary)
رَوَاهُ reported it rawāhu
الطَّبَرَانِيُّ aṭ-ṭabarānī aṭ-ṭabarānī
بِإِسْنَادَيْنِ with two chains bi isnādayn
أَحَدُهُمَا one of them aḥaduhumā
جَيِّدٌ reliable jayyid
At-Ṭabarānī recorded this with two chains, one being reliable (Jayyid). Al-Haythamī cited it in Majma‘uz-Zawā’id 10/120, and Al-Albānī authenticated it in Ṣaḥīḥ at-Targhīb 1/273.
✅ Source & Authenticity

Primary Source: Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim) § Morning & Evening Remembrances, invocation no. 98.

Ḥadīth origin: Narrated by At-Ṭabarānī in al-Mu‘jam aṣ-Ṣaghīr and al-Mu‘jam al-Awsaṭ. Al-Haythamī said one chain is Jayyid (reliable). Al-Albānī classified it as Ḥasan (good) in Ṣaḥīḥ at-Targhīb wat-Tarhīb (no. 1658).

Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ)

Full name: Abū al-Qāsim Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd Allāh ibn ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib ibn Hāshim (c. 570 CE – 632 CE).

The final messenger of Islam, whose intercession is sought for the believers on the Day of Judgment. This ḥadīth promises his intercession to those who abundantly invoke blessings upon him mornings and evenings.

Role here: Speaker of the original promise and central figure of the ṣalawāt.

Imam Sulaymān ibn Aḥmad Aṭ-Ṭabarānī (260–360 AH)

Full name: Abū al-Qāsim Sulaymān ibn Aḥmad ibn Ayyūb aṭ-Ṭabarānī. A major ḥadīth master (Ḥāfiẓ) and author of three Mu‘jam collections.

His transmission of this ḥadīth comes via two chains, one of which al-Haythamī graded Jayyid. He died in 360 AH / 971 CE.

Role here: Primary compiler and transmitter of the narration about morning/evening ṣalawāt.
