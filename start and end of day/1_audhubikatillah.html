Aʿūdhu bi Kalimāti Llāhi t-Tāmmāti

Protection Through the Perfect Words of Allah • Morning/Evening & Wayfarer’s Refuge

📖 Context of the Invocation

The Prophet ﷺ taught this du'ā as an all‑encompassing shield. When recited upon alighting at a resting place, nothing harms the person until they depart. It is also a powerful evening protection — one night a scorpion stung a Companion who had omitted it, and the Prophet ﷺ indicated that the recitation would have prevented the harm entirely.

📜 Narration Chain 1 · Khaula bint Ḥakīm (when staying at a place)

Khaula bint Ḥakīm as‑Sulamiyya reported directly from the Prophet ﷺ:
"When any one of you stays at a place, he should say: 'I seek refuge in the Perfect Words of Allah from the evil of that He created.' Nothing would then do him any harm until he moves from that place."

📜 Narration Chain 2 · Abū Hurayrah (evening recitation & scorpion sting)

Abū Hurayrah reported that a man came to the Prophet ﷺ and said:
"O Messenger of Allah, what I experienced from a scorpion that stung me last night!" Thereupon he said: "If you had said in the evening: 'I seek refuge in the Perfect Words of Allah from the evil of what He created,' it would not have harmed you."

✅ Source & Authenticity

Muslim, Book of Dhikr & Du'ā (Kitāb al‑Adhkār wa al‑Duʿā), no. 2708b (via Khaula bint Ḥakīm) and no. 2709a (via Abū Hurayrah). Both chains are graded Ṣaḥīḥ. The wording is identical: Aʿūdhu bi kalimāti llāhi t‑tāmmāti min sharri mā khalaq. Scholars recommend it for morning, evening, and when stopping during travel.
