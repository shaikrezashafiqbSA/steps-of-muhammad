Āyat at-Tawbah (9:128) & Ḥasbiyallāh Duʿā

The Prophet’s mercy described in the Quran & the morning reliance supplication

📖 Context & Virtue

Sūrah At-Tawbah 9:128 is among the final revealed verses, highlighting the immense compassion of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ for his community. The Ḥasbiyallāh duʿā (morning/evening) was taught by the Prophet ﷺ; whoever recites it seven times in the morning or evening, Allah will grant whatever he desires of this world and the Hereafter (Ibn as-Sunnī, Abū Dāwūd, authentic).

Part 2 · Ḥiṣn al-Muslim — Morning & Evening Reliance (7×)

The Narration Chain & Context (Ibn as-Sunnī, Abū Dāwūd)

Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ said:
“Whoever says in the morning and in the evening: Ḥasbiyallāhu lā ilāha illā huwa ʿalayhi tawakkaltu wa huwa rabbu l-ʿarshi l-ʿaẓīm seven times, Allah will suffice him for whatever concerns him of this world and the Hereafter.”

✅ Source & Authenticity (Duʿā)

Reported by Ibn As-Sunni in ‘Amal al-Yawm wa al-Laylah (no. 71) and by Abu Dawud (4/321) with a sound chain directly from the Prophet ﷺ (marfū‘). It is declared Ṣaḥīḥ by contemporary muhaddithūn. Reciting it seven times in the morning or evening brings divine sufficiency in all worldly and spiritual affairs.

Qur'an 9:128 – The Clear Quran
Prophet Muḥammad ibn ʿAbdillāh ﷺ

Full name: Abū al-Qāsim Muḥammad ibn ʿAbdillāh ibn ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib ibn Hāshim (c. 570 – 632 CE / 53 BH – 11 AH). The final messenger of Allāh, seal of the prophets, born in Makkah and buried in Madīnah.

He received the Qur’ān and taught the sunnah through words, actions, and approvals. His supplications are the most comprehensive and authentic guidance for daily worship. This particular ḥadīth (Ibn as-Sunnī, Abū Dāwūd) originates directly from his blessed instruction.

Role here: The originator of the duʿā and the command to recite it seven times in the morning and evening; his teaching guarantees Allāh’s sufficiency for the believer.
