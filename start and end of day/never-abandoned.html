اَللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ

Supplication for Forgiveness, Well‑Being, and Divine Protection – Morning & Evening

📖 Context of the Supplication

ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar (raḍiya Llāhu ʿanhumā) reported that the Prophet (ﷺ) never abandoned these words every morning and evening. They encapsulate a believer’s need for pardon, safety in both worlds, and complete protection from every direction of harm.

The Narration Chain & Context

Ibn ʿUmar reported:
"The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) never abandoned these supplications every evening and every morning..."
The Arabic Narration (Chain & Setup)
ʿAlī ibn Muḥammad aṭ-Ṭanāfisī narrated to us, Wakīʿ narrated to us, ʿUbādah ibn Muslim narrated to us, Jubayr ibn Abī Sulaymān ibn Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim narrated to us, he said: I heard Ibn ʿUmar saying: The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) would not leave these supplications when he entered the evening and when he entered the morning.

ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb (raḍiya Llāhu ʿanhumā)

Full name: ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb al-ʿAdawī (d. 73 AH / 692 CE). A senior Companion of the Prophet (ﷺ) and son of the second caliph ʿUmar. Renowned for his meticulous adherence to the Prophet’s practices and vast knowledge of ḥadīth.

He is among the most prolific narrators of ḥadīth, known for his piety, independent legal reasoning, and scrupulous avoidance of tribulations. His narrations are found in all major canonical collections, and his authority in transmitting supplications is universally accepted.

Role here: Direct transmitter of the Prophet’s daily morning/evening duʿā, preserving the exact wording and practice of the Messenger (ﷺ).

✅ Source & Authenticity

Recorded by Imām Ibn Mājah in Sunan Ibn Mājah, Kitāb ad-Duʿā’ (Book of Supplications), under “What a man should supplicate in the morning and evening.” The ḥadīth is graded Ṣaḥīḥ by Dārusalām. The chain is strong: ʿAlī ibn Muḥammad aṭ-Ṭanāfisī ← Wakīʿ ← ʿUbādah ibn Muslim ← Jubayr ibn Abī Sulaymān ← Ibn ʿUmar (raḍiya Llāhu ʿanhumā). The supplication was practiced daily by the Prophet (ﷺ) without exception.
