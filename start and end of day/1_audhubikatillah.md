# Protection Upon Arrival & In The Evening

## 📜 The Hadith

Khawlah bint Ḥakīm al-Sulamiyyah reported: "I heard Allah's Messenger ﷺ saying: 'When any one of you stays at a place, he should say: *\"I seek refuge in the Perfect Words of Allah from the evil of what He created.\"* Nothing would then do him any harm until he moves from that place.'" 

Abū Hurayrah reported that a person came to Allah's Messenger ﷺ and said: "O Messenger of Allah, I was stung by a scorpion during the night." Thereupon he said: "Had you recited these words in the evening: *\"I seek refuge in the Perfect Words of Allah from the evil of what He created,\"* it would not have done any harm to you."
