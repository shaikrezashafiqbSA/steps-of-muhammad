# دُعَاءُ الْحَمْد — The Supplication of Praise

## 📜 The Supplication

This comprehensive duʿāʾ opens with the praise of Allah, followed by abundant ṣalawāt upon the Prophet ﷺ, then a series of petitions for protection, well-being, and steadfastness. It is recited in gatherings of dhikr and after obligatory prayers — and most famously after the recitation of Sūrat Yāsīn — in the tradition of the Ba ʿAlawī scholars of Ḥaḍramawt, as transmitted through the Ḥabāʾib, including Ḥabīb ʿAbdullāh bin ʿAlawī al-Ḥaddād and the chains connected to Ḥabīb ʿAlī al-Ḥabashī and Ḥabīb ʿUmar bin Ḥafīẓ.

The worshipper begins: *"All praise is for Allah, Lord of all the worlds — praise that fulfills His favours and is commensurate with His increase. O our Lord, for You is all praise as befits the majesty of Your Face and the greatness of Your dominion. Glory be to You — we cannot enumerate praise upon You; You are as You have praised Yourself..."* — then sends blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ among the first and the last, entrusts to Allah his religion, soul, family and wealth, seeks His shelter from every rebellious devil, obstinate tyrant, evil eye, wrongdoing and envy, asks to be adorned with well-being and steadfastness, and seals with the Qur'anic verses of Sūrat aṣ-Ṣāffāt.

<details>
<summary>📚 Original Sources of the Composite Text </summary>

**No single isnād** — this is a compiled duʿāʾ, not one hadith. Its recited phrases trace to the following sources:

**1 · Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 486 — narrated by ʿĀʾishah (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhā):**
[[ لَا أُحْصِي ثَنَاءً عَلَيْكَ | I cannot enumerate praise upon You | lā uḥṣī thanāʾan ʿalayk ]]
[[ أَنْتَ كَمَا أَثْنَيْتَ عَلَى نَفْسِكَ | You are as You have praised Yourself | anta kamā athnayta ʿalā nafsik ]]
— The Prophet ﷺ said this in prostration during the night prayer. **Grade: Ṣaḥīḥ.**

**2 · Sunan Ibn Mājah 3801 — narrated by ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhumā):**
[[ يَا رَبِّ لَكَ الْحَمْدُ كَمَا يَنْبَغِي لِجَلَالِ وَجْهِكَ وَلِعَظِيمِ سُلْطَانِكَ | O Lord, for You is praise as befits the majesty of Your Face and the greatness of Your dominion | yā Rabbi laka-l-ḥamdu kamā yanbaghī li-jalāli wajhika wa li-ʿaẓīmi sulṭānik ]]
— A servant of Allah said this; the two recording angels could not encompass how to record it and ascended to ask their Lord, who said: *"Write it as My slave said it, until he meets Me and I reward him for it."* **Grade: Ḍaʿīf isnād** (al-Albānī; Islamweb Fatwā 65961), though widely recited.

**3 · "Ḥamdan yuwāfī niʿamahu wa yukāfiʾu mazīdah":**
Not an established Prophetic hadith. Reported (via Abū Naṣr at-Tammār) as words taught to Ādam (ʿalayhi-s-salām) — an Isrāʾīlī report, as Ibn al-Qayyim notes in *ʿUddat aṣ-Ṣābirīn*. Nevertheless regarded in the Shāfiʿī school as among the most sublime formulas of ḥamd, which is why it features across the awrād of the Ba ʿAlawī tradition.

**4 · "Nastaḥfiẓuka wa nastawdiʿuka..." (Parts 7–8):**
Transmitted in the duʿāʾ recited after Sūrat Yāsīn in the tradition of the Ḥabāʾib, commonly attributed to Ḥabīb ʿAbdullāh bin ʿAlawī al-Ḥaddād. Its theme echoes the authentic Prophetic farewell duʿāʾ "Allāhumma astawdiʿuka dīnahu wa amānatahu..." (Abū Dāwūd, at-Tirmidhī).

**5 · Qur'anic Seal — Sūrat aṣ-Ṣāffāt 37:180–182:**
[[ سُبْحَانَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ الْعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ | Glory be to your Lord, the Lord of Honour, above what they describe | subḥāna Rabbika Rabbi-l-ʿizzati ʿammā yaṣifūn ]]
[[ وَسَلَامٌ عَلَى الْمُرْسَلِينَ | and peace be upon the Messengers | wa salāmun ʿala-l-mursalīn ]]
[[ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ | and all praise is for Allah, Lord of the worlds | wa-l-ḥamdu lillāhi Rabbi-l-ʿālamīn ]]
— **Grade: Qur'anic Scripture.**

</details>

## 🔤 Word-by-Word

### Part 1 · Opening Basmala & Ḥamd

[[ بِسْمِ اللهِ | in the name of Allah | Bismi-llāh ]] [[ الرَّحْمَنِ | the Most Gracious | ar-Raḥmān ]] [[ الرَّحِيمِ | the Most Merciful, | ar-Raḥīm ]]

[[ الْحَمْدُ لِلهِ | all praise is for Allah, | al-ḥamdu lillāh ]] [[ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ | Lord of the worlds. | Rabbi-l-ʿālamīn ]]

### Part 2 · Praise that Befits His Bounty

[[ حَمْدًا | a praise | ḥamdan ]] [[ يُوَافِي | that fulfills | yuwāfī ]] [[ نِعَمَهُ | His favours | niʿamahu ]]

[[ وَيُكَافِئُ | and is commensurate with | wa yukāfiʾu ]] [[ مَزِيدَهُ | His increase. | mazīdah ]]

### Part 3 · Praise Befitting His Majesty

[[ يَا رَبَّنَا | O our Lord, | yā Rabbanā ]] [[ لَكَ الْحَمْدُ | for You is all praise | laka-l-ḥamd ]]

[[ كَمَا يَنْبَغِي | as is befitting | kamā yanbaghī ]] [[ لِجَلَالِ وَجْهِكَ | the majesty of Your Face | li-jalāli wajhik ]]

[[ وَعَظِيمِ سُلْطَانِكَ | and the greatness of Your dominion. | wa ʿaẓīmi sulṭānik ]]

**The Divine Guarantee** *(Sunan Ibn Mājah 3801 — the two recording angels could not encompass this phrase; Allah ﷻ said:)*

[[ اكْتُبَاهَا كَمَا قَالَ عَبْدِي | "Write it down as My slave said it, | uktubāhā kamā qāla ʿabdī ]]

[[ حَتَّى يَلْقَانِي | until he meets Me, | ḥattā yalqānī ]] [[ فَأَجْزِيهِ بِهَا | and I shall reward him for it." | fa-ajzīhi bihā ]]

*Note: this narration's chain is graded ḍaʿīf (al-Albānī), though the formula remains widely beloved and recited.*

### Part 4 · Glory Beyond All Enumeration *(Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 486)*

[[ سُبْحَانَكَ | glory be to You — | subḥānaka ]] [[ لَا نُحْصِي | we cannot enumerate | lā nuḥṣī ]] [[ ثَنَاءً عَلَيْكَ | praise upon You; | thanāʾan ʿalayk ]]

[[ أَنْتَ | You are | anta ]] [[ كَمَا أَثْنَيْتَ | as You have praised | kamā athnayta ]] [[ عَلَى نَفْسِكَ | upon Yourself. | ʿalā nafsik ]]

### Part 5 · Praise Until His Pleasure

[[ فَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ | so for You is praise | fa-laka-l-ḥamd ]] [[ حَتَّى تَرْضَى | until You are pleased, | ḥattā tarḍā ]]

[[ وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ | and for You is praise | wa laka-l-ḥamd ]] [[ إِذَا رَضِيتَ | when You are pleased, | idhā raḍīta ]]

[[ وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ | and for You is praise | wa laka-l-ḥamd ]] [[ بَعْدَ الرَّضَى | after the pleasure. | baʿda-r-raḍā ]]

### Part 6 · Ṣalawāt upon the Prophet ﷺ

[[ اللَّهُمَّ | O Allah, | Allāhumma ]] [[ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ | send blessings and peace | ṣalli wa sallim ]] [[ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا | upon our master | ʿalā Sayyidinā ]] [[ مُحَمَّدٍ | Muḥammad | Muḥammad ]] [[ فِي الْأَوَّلِينَ | among the first ones, | fi-l-awwalīn ]]

[[ وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ | and send blessings and peace | wa ṣalli wa sallim ]] [[ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ | upon our master Muḥammad | ʿalā Sayyidinā Muḥammad ]] [[ فِي الْآخِرِينَ | among the last ones, | fi-l-ākhirīn ]]

[[ وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ | and send blessings and peace | wa ṣalli wa sallim ]] [[ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ | upon our master Muḥammad | ʿalā Sayyidinā Muḥammad ]] [[ فِي كُلِّ وَقْتٍ | in every time | fī kulli waqt ]] [[ وَحِينٍ | and moment, | wa ḥīn ]]

[[ وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ | and send blessings and peace | wa ṣalli wa sallim ]] [[ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ | upon our master Muḥammad | ʿalā Sayyidinā Muḥammad ]] [[ فِي الْمَلَإِ الْأَعْلَى | in the highest assembly | fi-l-malaʾi-l-aʿlā ]] [[ إِلَى يَوْمِ الدِّينِ | until the Day of Judgement, | ilā yawmi-d-dīn ]]

[[ وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ | and send blessings and peace | wa ṣalli wa sallim ]] [[ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ | upon our master Muḥammad | ʿalā Sayyidinā Muḥammad ]]

[[ حَتَّى تَرِثَ الْأَرْضَ | until You inherit the earth | ḥattā taritha-l-arḍ ]] [[ وَمَنْ عَلَيْهَا | and whoever is upon it, | wa man ʿalayhā ]]

[[ وَأَنْتَ خَيْرُ | and You are the best | wa anta khayru ]] [[ الْوَارِثِينَ | of the inheritors. | al-wārithīn ]]

### Part 7 · Entrusting All Affairs to Allah

[[ اللَّهُمَّ | O Allah, | Allāhumma ]] [[ إِنَّا نَسْتَحْفِظُكَ | we ask You to safeguard | innā nastaḥfiẓuk ]] [[ وَنَسْتَوْدِعُكَ | and we entrust to You | wa nastawdiʿuk ]]

[[ أَدْيَانَنَا | our religion, | adyānanā ]] [[ وَأَنْفُسَنَا | our souls, | wa anfusanā ]] [[ وَأَمْوَالَنَا | our wealth, | wa amwālanā ]] [[ وَأَهْلَنَا | our families, | wa ahlanā ]]

[[ وَكُلَّ شَيْءٍ | and every thing | wa kulla shayʾ ]] [[ أَعْطَيْتَنَا | You have given us. | aʿṭaytanā ]]

### Part 8 · Seeking Protection

[[ اللَّهُمَّ | O Allah, | Allāhumma ]] [[ اجْعَلْنَا وَإِيَّاهُمْ | place us and them | ijʿalnā wa iyyāhum ]]

[[ فِي كَنَفِكَ | in Your shelter, | fī kanafik ]] [[ وَأَمَانِكَ | Your security, | wa amānik ]] [[ وَعِيَاذِكَ | and Your refuge, | wa ʿiyādhik ]]

[[ مِنْ كُلِّ شَيْطَانٍ | from every devil | min kulli shayṭān ]] [[ مَرِيدٍ | rebellious, | marīd ]]

[[ وَجَبَّارٍ عَنِيدٍ | and every obstinate tyrant, | wa jabbārin ʿanīd ]]

[[ وَذِي عَيْنٍ | and the possessor of the evil eye, | wa dhī ʿayn ]] [[ وَذِي بَغْيٍ | and of wrongdoing, | wa dhī baghī ]] [[ وَذِي حَسَدٍ | and of envy, | wa dhī ḥasad ]]

[[ وَمِنْ شَرِّ | and from the evil | wa min sharri ]] [[ كُلِّ ذِي شَرٍّ | of every evil-doer — | kulli dhī sharr ]]

[[ إِنَّكَ | verily You are | innaka ]] [[ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ | over every thing | ʿalā kulli shayʾ ]] [[ قَدِيرٌ | Powerful. | Qadīr ]]

### Part 9 · Well-Being & Steadfastness

[[ اللَّهُمَّ | O Allah, | Allāhumma ]] [[ جَمِّلْنَا | adorn us | jammilnā ]] [[ بِالْعَافِيَةِ | with well-being | bi-l-ʿāfiyah ]] [[ وَالسَّلَامَةِ | and safety, | wa-s-salāmah ]]

[[ وَحَقِّقْنَا | and grant us realisation | wa ḥaqqiqnā ]] [[ بِالتَّقْوَى | through piety | bi-t-taqwā ]] [[ وَالِاسْتِقَامَةِ | and steadfastness, | wa-l-istiqāmah ]]

[[ وَأَعِذْنَا | and protect us | wa aʿidhnā ]] [[ مِنْ مُوجِبَاتِ | from the causes of | min mūjibāti ]] [[ النَّدَامَةِ | regret | an-nadāmah ]]

[[ فِي الْحَالِ | in the present | fi-l-ḥāl ]] [[ وَالْمَآلِ | and the final outcome — | wa-l-maʾāl ]]

[[ إِنَّكَ | verily You are | innaka ]] [[ سَمِيعُ الدُّعَاءِ | the Hearer of supplication. | Samīʿu-d-duʿāʾ ]]

### Part 10 · Closing Ṣalawāt & Seal

[[ وَصَلِّ اللَّهُمَّ | and send blessings, O Allah, | wa ṣalli Allāhumma ]] [[ بِجَلَالِكَ | by Your majesty | bi-jalālik ]] [[ وَجَمَالِكَ | and Your beauty, | wa jamālik ]]

[[ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ | upon our master Muḥammad, | ʿalā Sayyidinā Muḥammad ]]

[[ وَعَلَى آلِهِ | and upon his family | wa ʿalā ālih ]] [[ وَصَحْبِهِ أَجْمَعِينَ | and all his companions, | wa ṣaḥbihī ajmaʿīn ]]

[[ وَارْزُقْنَا | and grant us | wa-rzuqnā ]] [[ كَمَالَ الْمُتَابَعَةِ | perfection of following | kamāla-l-mutābaʿah ]] [[ لَهُ | him, | lahu ]]

[[ ظَاهِرًا وَبَاطِنًا | outwardly and inwardly, | ẓāhiran wa bāṭinan ]]

[[ يَا أَرْحَمَ الرَّاحِمِينَ | O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. | yā Arḥama-r-Rāḥimīn ]]

### Part 11 · Qur'ānic Seal *(Sūrat aṣ-Ṣāffāt 37:180–182)*

[[ بِفَضْلِ | by the grace of: | bi-faḍli ]]

[[ سُبْحَانَ رَبِّكَ | Glory be to your Lord, | subḥāna Rabbika ]] [[ رَبِّ الْعِزَّةِ | the Lord of Honour, | Rabbi-l-ʿizzah ]] [[ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ | above what they describe, | ʿammā yaṣifūn ]]

[[ وَسَلَامٌ | and peace | wa salāmun ]] [[ عَلَى الْمُرْسَلِينَ | be upon the Messengers, | ʿala-l-mursalīn ]]

[[ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ | and all praise is for Allah, | wa-l-ḥamdu lillāh ]] [[ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ | Lord of the worlds. | Rabbi-l-ʿālamīn ]]

## ✅ Source & Authenticity

This supplication is a **compiled duʿāʾ** recited in the gatherings (majālis) of the Ba ʿAlawī tradition of Ḥaḍramawt as transmitted by the scholars of the Ḥabāʾib — it is **not a single hadith** and has no single isnād. Verified breakdown of its components:

- **"Lā nuḥṣī thanāʾan ʿalayk, anta kamā athnayta ʿalā nafsik"** — authentically established: **Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 486**, narrated by ʿĀʾishah (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhā), from the Prophet's ﷺ words in prostration.
- **"Laka-l-ḥamd kamā yanbaghī li-jalāli wajhika wa ʿaẓīmi sulṭānik"** — **Sunan Ibn Mājah 3801**, narrated by ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhumā), with the reward-narrative of the two angels. Its isnād is graded **ḍaʿīf** (al-Albānī; cf. Islamweb Fatāwā 65961 & 105601), though the wording remains widely recited as duʿāʾ.
- **"Ḥamdan yuwāfī niʿamahu wa yukāfiʾu mazīdah"** — **no established Prophetic isnād**. Ibn al-Qayyim (*ʿUddat aṣ-Ṣābirīn*) identifies it as an Isrāʾīlī report concerning Ādam (ʿalayhi-s-salām), transmitted via Abū Naṣr at-Tammār (cf. as-Saffārīnī, *Ghidhāʾ al-Albāb*). The Shāfiʿī school nonetheless regards it as among the most sublime formulas of ḥamd, which explains its prominence in Ba ʿAlawī awrād; some scholars (e.g., Ibn al-Qayyim, Bakr Abū Zayd) objected to it.
- **"Fa-laka-l-ḥamd ḥattā tarḍā..."** — a well-known formula of the awrād; I could not confirm a specific authenticated Prophetic isnād for this exact tripartite wording, so it should be treated as a transmitted devotional formula rather than a marfūʿ hadith.
- **Parts 7–9 ("nastaḥfiẓuka wa nastawdiʿuka...")** — transmitted in the duʿāʾ recited after Sūrat Yāsīn in the tradition of the Ḥabāʾib, commonly attributed to **Ḥabīb ʿAbdullāh bin ʿAlawī al-Ḥaddād**; thematically rooted in the authentic Prophetic entrustment duʿāʾ ("astawdiʿu-llāha dīnaka wa amānatak...").
- **Part 11** — **Qur'an, Sūrat aṣ-Ṣāffāt 37:180–182**.
- **Attribution note:** the compilation circulates in the majālis connected to Ḥabīb ʿAlī al-Ḥabashī and Ḥabīb ʿUmar bin Ḥafīẓ; however, a specific published citation for this exact compiled sequence under Ḥabīb ʿUmar's name could not be independently verified — treat the attribution as traditional transmission rather than a documented text reference.
