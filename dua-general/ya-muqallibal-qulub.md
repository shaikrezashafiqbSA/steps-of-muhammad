## Yā Muqallibal-Qulūb — The Prophet's ﷺ Most Frequent Supplication for a Steadfast Heart

### 📜 The Hadith
Anas ibn Mālik (رضي الله عنه) reported:
"The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) would often say: 
> *"O Changer of the Hearts! Strengthen my heart upon Your Religion."*
So I said: 'O Prophet of Allah! We believe in you and what you have come with, but do you fear for us?' He said: 'Yes. Indeed the hearts are between two Fingers of Allah's Fingers, He changes them as He wills.'"

### 🔄 Authentic Variations & Expansions

#### 1. The Expanded Version (Including Sight)
This variation adds a plea for the stability of both the heart and spiritual perception:
[[اللَّهُمَّ|O Allah,|allāhumma]] [[يَا|O|yā]] [[مُقَلِّبَ|Changer of|muqalliba]] [[الْقُلُوبِ|the hearts|l-qulūbi]] [[وَالْأَبْصَارِ|and the sights,|wa-l-abṣāri,]] [[ثَبِّتْ|make firm|thabbit]] [[قَلْبِي|my heart|qalbī]] [[عَلَىٰ|upon|ʿalā]] [[دِينِكَ|Your religion.|dīnika.]]

#### 2. The Plural Form (For Family & Community)
Used to encompass your family, loved ones, and the global Ummah:
[[يَا|O|yā]] [[مُقَلِّبَ|Changer of|muqalliba]] [[الْقُلُوبِ|the hearts,|l-qulūbi,]] [[ثَبِّتْ|make firm|thabbit]] [[قُلُوبَنَا|our hearts|qulūbanā]] [[عَلَىٰ|upon|ʿalā]] [[دِينِكَ|Your religion.|dīnika.]]

#### 3. Transitioning From Firmness to Active Obedience
Another highly attested authentic wording changes the core action from steadfastness (*thabbit*) to active alignment (*ṣarrif*) toward obedience, as reported in Sahih Muslim:
[[اللَّهُمَّ|O Allah,|allāhumma]] [[مُصَرِّفَ|Director of|muṣarrifa]] [[الْقُلُوبِ|the hearts,|l-qulūbi,]] [[صَرِّفْ|direct|ṣarrif]] [[قُلُوبَنَا|our hearts|qulūbanā]] [[عَلَىٰ|towards|ʿalā]] [[طَاعَتِكَ|Your obedience.|ṭāʿatika.]]
