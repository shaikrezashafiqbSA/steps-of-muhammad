# Protection from the Fire — Morning and Evening Supplication
## 📜 The Hadith
Al-Ḥārith ibn Muslim al-Tamīmī quoted his father Muslim ibn al-Ḥārith al-Tamīmī as saying that the **Messenger of Allah ﷺ told him secretly: 'When you finish the sunset prayer, say:'**

>*“O Allah, protect me from Hell” seven times; for if you say that and die that night, protection from it would be recorded for you; and when you finish the dawn prayer, say it in a similar way, for if you die that day, protection from it would be recorded for you.*
