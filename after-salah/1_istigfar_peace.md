# The Post-Prayer Seeking of Forgiveness and Declaration of Peace
## 📜 The Hadith
Thauban reported: When the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) finished his prayer, he begged forgiveness three times and said: **"O Allah! You are peace, and peace comes from You; Blessed are you, Possessor of Glory and Honour."** Walid reported: I said to al-Awzāʿī: "How is the seeking of forgiveness?" He replied: **"You should say: I beg forgiveness from Allah, I beg forgiveness from Allah."**
