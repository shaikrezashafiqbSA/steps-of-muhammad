## Allāhumma Antas-Salām — The Post-Prayer Seeking of Forgiveness and Declaration of Peace

### 📜 The Hadith
Thawbān (رضي الله عنه) reported: 
When the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) finished his prayer, he begged forgiveness three times and said: 
> *"O Allah! You are peace, and peace comes from You; Blessed are You, Possessor of Glory and Honour."*

Al-Walīd reported: I said to al-Awzāʿī: "How is the seeking of forgiveness?" He replied: "You should say: *I beg forgiveness from Allah, I beg forgiveness from Allah.*"
