# Tasbīḥ, Taḥmīd & Takbīr — The Foam of the Sea

## 📜 The Hadith

Abū Hurayrah (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu) reported that some poor Emigrants came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and said the wealthy had attained the highest ranks and everlasting bliss through their wealth. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Shall I not teach you something by means of which you will catch up with those who have surpassed you and by which you will supersede those who come after you...? **Extol Allah (say Subḥān Allāh) at the end of every prescribed prayer thirty-three times, praise Allah (say Al-Ḥamdu Lillāh) thirty-three times, and glorify Allah (say Allāhu Akbar) thirty-four times.**"

> "Whoever recites these after every prayer — his sins are forgiven even if they were like the foam of the sea." — Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 1410.
