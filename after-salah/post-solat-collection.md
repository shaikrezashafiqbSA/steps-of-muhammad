# Post-Ṣalāh Collection — After Every Prayer

A curated sequence to recite immediately after the taslīm, drawing from multiple authentic narrations. Recite top to bottom; items marked *optional* or *Fajr/Maghrib only* are noted inline. Each item below is independently sourced and gradeable — expand any card to see its full narration, Arabic, and word-by-word.

## 📿 The Sequence

1. **Istighfār & Salām** — [Allāhumma Antas-Salām](?file=after-salah/1_istigfar_peace.md)
   *Muslim 591 · Sahih*

2. **Tahlīl — Sincere Devotion** — [Lā Ilāha Illā Allāh (Mukhlisina lahud-dīn)](?file=after-salah/tahlil-mukhlisina.md)
   *Sunan an-Nasāʾī 1339 · Sahih (Darussalam)*

3. **Tahlīl — Divine Sovereignty** *(optional)* — [Lā Māniʿa Limā Aʿṭayta](?file=after-salah/tahlil-la-mania.md)
   *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 593a · Sahih*

4. **Tahlīl × 10** *(Fajr & Maghrib only)* — [Lā Ilāha Illallāh Waḥdahū](?file=after-salah/tahlil-fajr-maghrib-10-times.md)
   *Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 3474 · Hasan Gharīb*

5. **Tasbīḥ, Taḥmīd & Takbīr × 33/33/34** — [Subḥān Allāh · Al-Ḥamdu Lillāh · Allāhu Akbar](?file=after-salah/tasbih-33-33-34.md)
   *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 1410 · Sahih*
