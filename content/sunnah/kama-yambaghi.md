# The Praise the Angels Could Not Write Down

## 📜 The Hadith

It was narrated from ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar ؓ that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ told them: "One of the slaves of Allah said:  *O Lord, to You is praise as befits the Glory of Your Face and the greatness of Your Might.* The two angels were uncertain and did not know how to write it down, so they ascended to heaven and said: 'O our Lord, Your slave has said a word that we do not know how to write down.' Allah said — and He knows best what His slave said — 'What did My slave say?' They said: <Dua below> 

Allah, Mighty and Majestic, said to them: 'Write it down as My slave said it, until he meets Me and I shall reward him for it.'"

## الضَّمَان ۞ Allah's Guarantee

The condition: the slave uttered this praise. The stated outcome — Allah commands it recorded verbatim and pledges to reward him for it when they meet:

[[ اكْتُبَاهَا | write it down | uktubāhā ]] [[ كَمَا | as | kamā ]] [[ قَالَ | said | qāla ]] [[ عَبْدِي | My slave | ʿabdī ]] [[ حَتَّى | until | ḥattā ]] [[ يَلْقَانِي | he meets Me | yalqānī ]] [[ فَأَجْزِيَهُ | so I reward him | fa-ajziyahū ]] [[ بِهَا | for it | bihā ]]

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Textual.** This reading follows from the wording of the text itself. (Note: the chain is graded Ḍaʿīf by Darussalam, so all of the below is reflection on the wording, not established prophetic teaching.)

The text stages a striking reversal: the recording angels — whose entire task is to write — are stopped by a single sentence from a human tongue. The phrase [[ كَمَا يَنْبَغِي | as befits | kamā yanbaghī ]] surrenders the scale of the praise back to Allah Himself; the slave does not measure Allah's glory, he confesses that only Allah's Face and Might can measure it. That is precisely what overwhelms the scribes: a praise whose weight is defined by the Praised, not the praiser.

Equally deliberate is the line [[ وَهُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِمَا قَالَ عَبْدُهُ | and He knows best what His slave said | wa-huwa aʿlamu bimā qāla ʿabduhū ]]. Allah asks the angels to repeat the words not out of need, but so that the slave's sentence is spoken once on earth and again in heaven — the same praise ascends with the very angels who record deeds. And the outcome is deferred to the meeting itself: [[ حَتَّى يَلْقَانِي | until he meets Me | ḥattā yalqānī ]] ties the reward not to this world but to the encounter every worshipper longs for.

</details>
