# What is the Duha prayer offered in the morning?

## ⏱️ The Ḍuḥā Window
Opens: **15 to 20 minutes after sunrise/ shuruq** - once the sun has visibly cleared the horizon. 

Closes: **15 to 20 minutes before dhuhr** - just before the sun reaches its highest point. 

2 rak'ah or 4 rakʿah, prayed two by two. Choose how much you want to hold onto the truth:
