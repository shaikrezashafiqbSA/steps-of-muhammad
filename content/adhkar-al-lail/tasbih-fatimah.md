# Tasbīḥ Fāṭimah — Better For You Both Than a Servant

## 📜 The Hadith
ʿAlī (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu) reported that Fāṭimah (ʿalayhā s-salām) complained of the blisters the hand-mill left on her hands, and went to ask the Prophet ﷺ for a servant from the captives that had come. She did not find him; she told ʿĀʾishah. When he came he sat between them on their bed, so close that ʿAlī said, *"I found the coolness of his feet on my chest,"* and said:

**"Shall I not teach you something better than what you asked me for? When you take to your beds, say *Allāhu akbar* thirty-four times, *subḥāna llāh* thirty-three times, and *al-ḥamdu lillāh* thirty-three times — that is better for you both than a servant."**

He did not give her the help she asked for. He gave her something to say instead, and called it the better of the two.

## 🧮 The Count
One hundred in total. The Bukhārī wording lists **takbīr 34 first**, then tasbīḥ 33, then taḥmīd 33; other authentic narrations order it tasbīḥ · taḥmīd · takbīr. Either order is transmitted — the count is the point.
