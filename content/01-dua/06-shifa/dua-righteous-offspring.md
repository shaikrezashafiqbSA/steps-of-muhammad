Duʿāʾ for Righteous Offspring – Calling upon Allah by His Beautiful Names

This duʿāʾ combines the supplication of Prophet Zakariyyā (ʿalayhi al-salām) with invocations of Allah’s Beautiful Names (al-Asmāʾ al-Ḥusnā) that are especially relevant when asking for children—whether you are trying to conceive, seeking protection for your children, or praying for their guidance and righteousness.

Translation

“My Lord, grant me from Yourself righteous offspring. Indeed, You are the Hearer of supplication.” (Qurʾān 3:38)

“O Bestower, O Provider, O Creator, O All-Powerful, O Responder, O Most Merciful, O One Who Brings Forward, and O One Who Delays — bless me with righteous offspring who will be the coolness of my eyes, a source of goodness in this world, and a means of reward in the Hereafter.

O Allah, bring this blessing forward by Your mercy. And if You delay it by Your wisdom, make me patient, content with Your decree, and steadfast in my hope in You. Indeed, You are the Most Generous, the Most Wise.”

Notes & Context

Attribution

The core duʿāʾ — “Rabbi hab lī min ladunka dhurriyyatan ṭayyibah, innaka samīʿu al-duʿāʾ” — is the supplication of Prophet Zakariyyā (Zachariah) as recorded in the Qurʾān. Zakariyyā called upon Allah using His name Al-Wahhāb (The Bestower), recognising that children are a gift from Allah alone.

The Names of Allah in This Duʿāʾ

Name Meaning Qurʾānic / Sunnah Reference
Al-Khāliq (The Creator) The One who brings things into existence from nothing Mentioned in multiple Qurʾānic verses including 6:102, 13:16, 39:62, 40:62, 59:24
Al-Wahhāb (The Bestower) The One who gives gifts freely and without measure Mentioned three times in the Qurʾān
Ar-Razzāq (The Provider) The One who grants sustenance to all creation Mentioned in the Qurʾān (e.g., 51:58)
Al-Qādir (The All-Powerful) The All-Able, The All-Capable Referenced in the Qurʾān
Al-Mujīb (The Responder) The One who answers the prayers of those who call upon Him “Indeed, my Lord is near and responsive” (Qurʾān 11:61)
Al-Ḥāfiẓ (The Protector) The Guardian and Preserver of all things “Indeed, my Lord is Guardian (Al-Ḥāfiẓ) over all things” (Qurʾān 11:57)
Al-Hādī (The Guide) The One who bestows guidance “Verily Allah always guides those who believe to the straight path” (Qurʾān 22:54)
Al-Muqaddim (The One Who Brings Forward) & Al-Muʾakhkhir (The One Who Delays) He brings forward and delays according to His perfect wisdom Mentioned together in the ḥadīth of the Prophet ﷺ: “You are al-Muqaddim, and You are al-Muʾakhkhir” (Bukhārī & Muslim)

How to Recite

Recite this duʿāʾ with sincerity and conviction, especially:

· When trying to conceive or struggling with infertility
· When pregnant, asking for protection and righteousness for the child
· When your children are young, seeking their protection and guidance
· At any time, as the duʿāʾ of Zakariyyā is timeless and can be recited repeatedly

The Prophet ﷺ also taught a specific protection before intimacy:

“In the name of Allah. O Allah! Protect us from Satan and prevent Satan from approaching the offspring You are going to give us.”

Benefits Mentioned

· Calling upon Allah by His Beautiful Names brings the believer closer to Allah and increases the likelihood of acceptance
· The duʿāʾ of Zakariyyā was answered despite his old age and apparent impossibility—a testament to Allah’s power as Al-Qādir
· Allah’s delay (as Al-Muʾakhkhir) is never random; it is part of His perfect plan, and the believer is encouraged to remain patient and hopeful

<summary>Chain of Narration / Isnād for the Ḥadīth of Al-Muqaddim &amp; Al-Muʾakhkhir</summary>

The ḥadīth is reported by Abū Mūsā (may Allah be pleased with him) and recorded by both Al-Bukhārī and Muslim (Muttafaqun ʿalayh).

The full duʿāʾ of the Prophet ﷺ:

“Allāhumma ighfir lī khaṭīʾatī wa-jahlī, wa-isrāfī fī amrī, wa-mā anta aʿlamu bihī minnī. Allāhumma ighfir lī jiddī wa-hazlī, wa-khaṭaʾī wa-ʿamdī, wa-kullu dhālika ʿindī. Allāhumma ighfir lī mā qaddamtu wa-mā akhkhartu, wa-mā asrartu wa-mā aʿlantu, wa-mā anta aʿlamu bihī minnī. Anta al-Muqaddim, wa-Anta al-Muʾakhkhir, wa-Anta ʿalā kulli shayʾin Qadīr.”

“O Allah, forgive me my sin, my ignorance, and my excess in my affairs, and whatever You know better about me than I do. O Allah, forgive me my seriousness and my jesting, my error and my deliberate acts, and all of that is with me. O Allah, forgive me what I have done in the past and what I will do in the future, what I have concealed and what I have done openly, and whatever You know better about me than I do. You are al-Muqaddim, and You are al-Muʾakhkhir, and You are over all things All-Powerful.”
