# Seeking Refuge for Children: The Du'a of the Prophet ﷺ for Ḥasan and Ḥusayn

Ibn ʿAbbās ؓ narrated that the Prophet ﷺ used to **seek refuge for Ḥasan and Ḥusayn**, saying:

> *"I seek refuge for you both in the Perfect Words of Allah, from every devil and every poisonous reptile, and from every evil eye."*

He then said: *"Thus Ibrāhīm ؑ used to seek refuge with Allah for Ismāʿīl and Isḥāq,"* or he said: *"for Ismāʿīl and Yaʿqūb."*
