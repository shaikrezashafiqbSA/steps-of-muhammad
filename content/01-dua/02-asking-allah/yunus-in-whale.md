# Yunus' Dua in the Whale - When there is no way out

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said that the supplication of Dhun-Nun (Prophet Yūnus) when he called out **while in the belly of the whale** was this, and that **no Muslim man supplicates with it for anything**.
> *"There is none worthy of worship except You, Glory to You, indeed I have been of the transgressors."*

[[ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ | Glory be to You! | subḥānaka ]]

**Scholarly Documentation & Chain Reality**

- **Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī:** Recorded in Chapters on Supplication (No. 3505). In-book reference: Book 48, Hadith 136.
- **Grade:** Ṣaḥīḥ (Darussalam).
- **Note by at-Tirmidhī:** More than one narrator reported this ḥadīth from Yūnus ibn Abī Isḥāq, from Ibrāhīm ibn Muḥammad ibn Saʿd, from Saʿd, without mentioning "from his father". Others reported it with "from his father". Yūnus ibn Abī Isḥāq sometimes mentioned "from his father" in this ḥadīth and sometimes did not.
