# After the Taslīm — The Prophet's ﷺ Closing Practices

After completing the obligatory ṣalāh with the taslīm, the Prophet ﷺ taught multiple duʿās for different states of the heart. Each was witnessed by different Companions. All are authentic; they are complementary practices, not alternatives.

## 🎯 The Prophetic Post-Taslīm Set

### For Affirming Sincere Devotion


**[→ Lā Ilāha Illā Allāh — The Closing Tahlīl](?file=after-salah/tahlil-mukhlisina.md)**  


*Narrated by ʿAbdullāh ibn al-Zubayr · Sunan an-Nasāʾī 1339 · Ṣaḥīḥ*

The Prophet ﷺ would proclaim Allah's oneness, affirm His sovereignty, and declare sincere devotion even though disbelievers detest it.

### For Trusting Divine Sovereignty


**[→ Lā Māniʿa Limā Aʿṭayta](?file=after-salah/tahlil-la-mania.md)**  


*Narrated by al-Mughīra ibn Shuʿba · Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 593a · Ṣaḥīḥ*

The Prophet ﷺ would affirm that none can prevent what Allah gives, none can give what He withholds, and worldly status cannot avail before Him.

---

## 📖 A Note On Multiple Variants

These are not contradictions. The Prophet ﷺ practiced many different post-taslīm devotions across his life, and different Companions recorded what they witnessed. Some scholars (al-Nawawī) suggest reciting one or several based on time and inner state. Others (Ibn al-Qayyim) recommend a flowing combination.

*All variants above are independently verifiable to canonical sources.*
