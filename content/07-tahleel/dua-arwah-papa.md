# Tahlil Du'a: Praise, Ṣalawāt and Du'a for the Deceased

This is the closing du'a recited at the end of a tahlil gathering compiled by Haji Abdul Sajeed Al Kampung Sembawang. It opens with praise of Allah and ṣalawāt on the Prophet ﷺ, presents the reward of what was recited to the Prophet ﷺ, the prophets, the Companions, the scholars and the deceased, and ends with du'a for those in the graves. The line asking forgiveness for the deceased is said **three times**.

*Pointer only: the wording is not verified — a place to consult, not a quotation.*

**Ḥadīth ✦ Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 2460**

- **Recorded:** Chapters on the Day of Judgement and ar-Riqāq, narrated by Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī ؓ. Contains the statement that the grave is either a garden from the gardens of Paradise or a pit from the pits of the Fire.
- **Grade:** At-Tirmidhī: gharīb. Darussalam: ḍaʿīf. Al-Albānī and Aḥmad Shākir: very ḍaʿīf. Zubair ʿAlī Zaʾī: isnād ḍaʿīf. HadithAnswers notes that parts of the ḥadīth are supported by other narrations.

*🔗 Verify on sunnah.com — [Jami` at-Tirmidhi 2460](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:2460)*

**📄 Page 9**

- "Allāhumma ighfir lahum wa-irḥamhum wa-ʿāfihim wa-ʿfu ʿanhum … kamā yunaqqā th-thawbu l-abyaḍu mina d-danas" → Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 963

**Ḥadīth ✦ Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 963**

- **Recorded:** Book of Funerals, chapter "Supplicating for the deceased during the funeral prayer", narrated by ʿAwf ibn Mālik ؓ.
- **Parallels:** Sunan an-Nasāʾī 62 and 1983, Sunan Ibn Mājah 1500 (ṣaḥīḥ per Darussalam), Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 1025 (shorter).
- **Differs:** The ḥadīth says "mina l-khaṭāyā"; the recital reads "mina dh-dhunūbi wa-l-khaṭāyā". The ḥadīth is for one deceased person (singular), the recital for several.

*🔗 Verify on sunnah.com — [Sahih Muslim 963](https://sunnah.com/muslim:963a)*

**Ḥadīth ✦ Sunan Abī Dāwūd 1495**

- **Recorded:** Book of Witr, chapter "Regarding Supplication", narrated by Anas ibn Mālik ؓ. A man supplicates by the fact that all praise is due to Allah, calling Him al-Mannān, Badīʿ as-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ, Dhū l-jalāli wa-l-ikrām, al-Ḥayy, al-Qayyūm, and the Prophet ﷺ says he has called upon Allah by His greatest name.
- **Parallels:** Sunan an-Nasāʾī 1300, Sunan Ibn Mājah 3858, Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 3544.
- **Differs:** "Yā ḥannān" is not in the reported wording.
- **Grade:** Ṣaḥīḥ per al-Albānī. Ṣaḥīḥ per Shuʿayb al-Arnaʾūṭ.

*🔗 Verify on sunnah.com — [Sunan Abi Dawud 1495](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1495)*

**Ḥadīth ✦ Sunan Abī Dāwūd 3201**

- **Recorded:** Book of Funerals, chapter "The Supplication for the Deceased", narrated by Abū Hurayrah ؓ. The Prophet ﷺ, at a funeral prayer, asks Allah not to deprive those present of the reward and not to lead them astray after the deceased.
- **Related report (plural wording):** Musnad Aḥmad 24425, narrated by ʿĀʾishah ؓ, where the Prophet ﷺ speaks at al-Baqīʿ with "lā taḥrimnā ajrahum wa-lā taftinnā baʿdahum". Shuʿayb al-Arnaʾūṭ: the isnād is ḍaʿīf in this wording.
- **Differs:** The last clause varies between reports ("tuḍillanā" / "taftinnā").
- **Grade:** Abū Dāwūd 3201: ṣaḥīḥ per al-Albānī.
