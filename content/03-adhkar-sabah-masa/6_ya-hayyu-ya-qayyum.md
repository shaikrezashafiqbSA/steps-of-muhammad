# Duʿāʾ of Fāṭimah - Entrust every affair to the Ever-Living

Anas ibn Mālik (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu) reported that the Prophet ﷺ said to Fāṭimah (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhā): "What prevents you from listening to what I advise you to say? Say, when you enter the morning and when you enter the evening: *'O Ever-Living, O Sustainer, by Your mercy I seek relief. Set right for me all my affairs, and do not leave me to myself even for the blink of an eye.'*"

[[بِرَحْمَتِكَ|by Your mercy|bi-raḥmatika]] [[أَسْتَغِيثُ|I seek relief,|astaghīthu]]

[[ أَصْلِحْ | set right | aṣliḥ ]] [[ لِي | for me | lī ]] [[ شَأْنِي | my affairs | sha'nī ]] [[ كُلَّهُ | all of them, | kullahū ]]

**Virtue & Significance**

*This narration attaches no explicit reward or promised outcome. Its weight comes from the framing: the Prophet ﷺ personally urged his own daughter Fāṭimah (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhā) to keep this du'a morning and evening — a mark of how much he valued it.*
