# Praising Allah After Eating — Forgiveness of Past Sins

## 📜 The Hadith 

Muʿādh ibn Anas ؓ reported: The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said, "Whoever eats food and then says: *Al-ḥamdu lillāhi-lladhī aṭʿamanī hādhā, wa razaqanīhi min ghayri ḥawlin minnī wa lā quwwatin* — 'All praise is due to Allah Who has fed me this and provided it for me without any might or power on my part' — his past sins will be forgiven."

## الضَّمَان ۞ Allah's Guarantee

The text attaches an explicit outcome to the act of praising Allah after eating: the one who says these words after food has his past sins forgiven.

The condition:

[[ مَنْ | Whoever | man ]]
[[ أَكَلَ | eats | akala ]]
[[ طَعَامًا | food | ṭaʿāman ]]
[[ فَقَالَ | then says | fa-qāla ]]

The result:

[[ غُفِرَ | will be forgiven | ghufira ]]
[[ لَهُ | for him | lahu ]]
[[ مَا | what | mā ]]
[[ تَقَدَّمَ | preceded | taqaddama ]]
[[ مِنْ | of | min ]]
[[ ذَنْبِهِ | his sin | dhanbihi ]]

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Textual.** This reading follows from the wording of the text itself.

The supplication is structured in two movements: an act of praise [[ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ | All praise is due to Allah | al-ḥamdu lillāhi ]] followed by a confession of inability [[ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلَا قُوَّةٍ | without any might or power on my part | min ghayri ḥawlin minnī wa-lā quwwatin ]]. The text pairs gratitude upward with humility inward — the eater names Allah as the true provider while explicitly disowning the two things people most often credit themselves with: strength (ḥawl) and capability (quwwah).

The wording of the result mirrors this: the passive verb [[ غُفِرَ | it was forgiven | ghufira ]] carries no mention of the eater's effort, just as the duʿāʾ carried no mention of the eater's power. What is given without the servant's might is matched by forgiveness without the servant's earning — the structure of the reward echoes the structure of the words.

</details>
