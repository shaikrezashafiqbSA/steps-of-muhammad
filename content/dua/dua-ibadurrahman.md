# ʿIbād ar-Raḥmān: The Servants of the Most Gracious

## 📜 The Qur'anic Passage

Allah names a people He calls His own servants, and then He tells you exactly who they are. Ten traits, two duas, one road back for anyone who has fallen, and one reward. Every ayah below is kept whole and every word carries its full marks. Tick each step as you make it yours.


### ✅ The ʿIbād ar-Raḥmān Checklist

- [ ] 1 · Walk the earth with humility
- [ ] 2 · Answer the ignorant with peace
- [ ] 3 · Stand and prostrate before your Lord at night
- [ ] 4 · Ask Allah to turn Hell away from you
- [ ] 5 · Spend without waste and without stinginess
- [ ] 6 · Hold the three lines: no shirk, no unlawful killing, no zinā
- [ ] 7 · Return to Allah in tawbah
- [ ] 8 · Stay away from falsehood and pass vain talk with dignity
- [ ] 9 · Stay alive to the verses of your Lord
- [ ] 10 · Ask Allah for a family who are the comfort of your eyes

## Steps 1 and 2 · Walk Humbly, Answer with Peace

### Ayah 63

The ˹true˺ servants of the Most Compassionate are those who walk on the earth humbly, and when the foolish address them ˹improperly˺, they only respond with peace. (25:63)

[[ وَعِبَادُ | and the servants | wa-ʿibādu ]] [[ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ | of the Most Gracious | ar-raḥmāni ]] [[ ٱلَّذِينَ | are those who | alladhīna ]] [[ يَمۡشُونَ | walk | yamshūna ]] [[ عَلَى | upon | ʿalā ]] [[ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ | the earth | al-arḍi ]] [[ هَوۡنٗا | in humility, | hawnan ]] [[ وَإِذَا | and when | wa-idhā ]] [[ خَاطَبَهُمُ | address them | khāṭabahumu ]] [[ ٱلۡجَٰهِلُونَ | the ignorant, | al-jāhilūna ]] [[ قَالُواْ | they say, | qālū ]] [[ سَلَٰمٗا | "Peace." | salāmā ]] [[ ۝٦٣ ]]

**Tick when done**
- [ ] Walk with calm and dignity, never with swagger
- [ ] Come to prayer with tranquility, never rushing
- [ ] Do not answer insult with insult
- [ ] Forgive, overlook, and say only good words

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Commentary.** Attributed to Tafsīr al-Qur'ān al-ʿAẓīm, Ibn Kathīr. Verify the wording against the original before citing.

**Humility is not a performance.** Ibn Kathīr explains that they walk with dignity and humility, not with arrogance and pride, and links it to 17:37, which forbids walking on the earth with conceit. He then draws the line the other way: it does not mean walking like a sick person making a show of humility. The Prophet ﷺ walked as if coming downhill, as if the earth were folded beneath him.

**What [[ هَوۡنٗا | humility | hawnan ]] means.** Ibn Kathīr defines it as serenity and dignity. He cites the Prophet's ﷺ instruction for coming to prayer: do not come rushing in haste, come with calm and tranquility, pray what you catch and make up what you miss.

**What "peace" means here.** When ignorant people insult them with bad words, they do not respond in kind. They forgive, overlook, and say nothing but good words. Ibn Kathīr adds that this is what the Messenger of Allah ﷺ did: the more ignorant the people were with him, the more patient he became. He connects it to 28:55, where believers who hear vain talk withdraw from it.

</details>

## Step 3 · Stand and Prostrate at Night

### Ayah 64

˹They are˺ those who spend ˹a good portion of˺ the night, prostrating themselves and standing before their Lord. (25:64)

[[ وَٱلَّذِينَ | and those who | wa-alladhīna ]] [[ يَبِيتُونَ | spend the night | yabītūna ]] [[ لِرَبِّهِمۡ | for their Lord | li-rabbihim ]] [[ سُجَّدٗا | prostrating | sujjadan ]] [[ وَقِيَٰمٗا | and standing. | wa-qiyāman ]] [[۝٦٤]]

**Tick when done**
- [ ] Give a portion of the night to your Lord
- [ ] Stand in prayer
- [ ] Prostrate in prayer

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Commentary.** Attributed to Tafsīr al-Qur'ān al-ʿAẓīm, Ibn Kathīr. Verify the wording against the original before citing.

**Their nights are the best of nights.** Ibn Kathīr introduces this verse that way: the night is spent worshipping and obeying Allah. He gathers three parallel verses: they slept little by night and asked forgiveness before dawn (51:17-18), their sides forsake their beds (32:16), and the one obedient through the night prostrating and standing, fearing the Hereafter and hoping for his Lord's mercy (39:9).

</details>

## Step 4 · Recite This Dua: Turn Hell Away

🤲 Two ayahs, one dua. Recite them together.

### Ayah 65


˹They are˺ those who pray, *"Our Lord! Keep the punishment of Hell away from us, for its punishment is indeed unrelenting."* (25:65)

[[ وَٱلَّذِينَ | and those who | wa-alladhīna ]] [[ يَقُولُونَ | say, | yaqūlūna ]] [[ رَبَّنَا | Our Lord, | rabbanā ]] [[ ٱصۡرِفۡ | turn away | iṣrif ]] [[ عَنَّا | from us | ʿannā ]] [[ عَذَابَ | the punishment | ʿadhāba ]] [[ جَهَنَّمَۖ | of Hell. | jahannama ]] [[ إِنَّ | Indeed, | inna ]] [[ عَذَابَهَا | its punishment | ʿadhābahā ]] [[ كَانَ | is | kāna ]] [[ غَرَامًا | unrelenting. | gharāmā ]] [[۝٦٥]]

### Ayah 66

*"It is certainly an evil place to settle and reside."* (25:66)

[[ إِنَّهَا | Indeed, it | innahā ]] [[ سَآءَتۡ | is evil | sāʾat ]] [[ مُسۡتَقَرّٗا | as a place to settle | mustaqarran ]] [[ وَمُقَامٗا | and to reside. | wa-muqāmā ]] [[۝٦٦]]

**Tick when done**
- [ ] Learn the dua by heart
- [ ] Recite it after your night prayer
- [ ] Ask with fear and with hope together

**The people who fear most are the people who pray most.** This dua sits directly after those who spend the night prostrating and standing. Ibn Kathīr links them to 39:9, the worshipper of the night who [[ يَحْذَرُ | fears | yaḥdharu ]] [[ الْآخِرَةَ | the Hereafter | al-ākhirata ]] [[ وَيَرْجُو | and hopes for | wa-yarjū ]] [[ رَحْمَةَ | the mercy | raḥmata ]] [[ رَبِّهِ | of his Lord. | rabbihi ]] Their night prayer does not make them feel safe. It makes them ask.

**One word separates this world from the next.** Ibn Kathīr reports al-Ḥasan on [[ غَرَامًا | unrelenting | gharāmā ]]: whatever strikes the son of Adam and then disappears is not gharām. Gharām is what lasts as long as heaven and earth. Sulaymān at-Taymī held the same view. Every hardship you carry today has an end. This dua asks protection from the one that does not.

**The answer mirrors the request.** The servants describe Hell as [[ سَآءَتۡ | evil | sāʾat ]] [[ مُسۡتَقَرّٗا | as a settlement | mustaqarran ]] [[ وَمُقَامٗا | and residence. | wa-muqāmā ]] Ten verses later, Allah describes their reward as [[ حَسُنَتۡ | excellent | ḥasunat ]] [[ مُسۡتَقَرّٗا | as a settlement | mustaqarran ]] [[ وَمُقَامٗا | and residence. | wa-muqāmā ]] The same two nouns. One verb changes.

## Step 5 · Spend in Balance

### Ayah 67

˹They are˺ those who spend neither wastefully nor stingily, but moderately in between. (25:67)

[[ وَٱلَّذِينَ | and those who | wa-alladhīna ]] [[ إِذَآ | when | idhā ]] [[ أَنفَقُواْ | they spend | anfaqū ]] [[ لَمۡ | do not | lam ]] [[ يُسۡرِفُواْ | waste | yusrifū ]] [[ وَلَمۡ | and do not | wa-lam ]] [[ يَقۡتُرُواْ | stint, | yaqturū ]] [[ وَكَانَ | and it is | wa-kāna ]] [[ بَيۡنَ | between | bayna ]] [[ ذَٰلِكَ | that | dhālika ]] [[ قَوَامٗا | a just balance. | qawāmā ]] [[۝٦٧]]

**Tick when done**
- [ ] Spend on what you need, not beyond it
- [ ] Provide for your family's needs in full
- [ ] Choose the middle path in every purchase

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Commentary.** Attributed to Tafsīr al-Qur'ān al-ʿAẓīm, Ibn Kathīr. Verify the wording against the original before citing.

**Two failures, one road between them.** Ibn Kathīr says they are not extravagant, spending more than they need, and not miserly towards their families by failing to spend enough on their needs. They follow the best and fairest way, because the best of matters are the moderate ones. He ties it to 17:29: do not let your hand be tied to your neck, nor stretch it out to its utmost reach.

</details>

## Step 6 · Hold the Three Lines

### Ayah 68

˹They are˺ those who do not invoke any other god besides Allah, nor take a ˹human˺ life, made sacred by Allah, except with ˹legal˺ right, nor commit fornication. And whoever does ˹any of˺ this will face the penalty. (25:68)

[[ وَٱلَّذِينَ | and those who | wa-alladhīna ]] [[ لَا | do not | lā ]] [[ يَدۡعُونَ | invoke | yadʿūna ]] [[ مَعَ | alongside | maʿa ]] [[ ٱللَّهِ | Allah | allāhi ]] [[ إِلَٰهًا | a god | ilāhan ]] [[ ءَاخَرَ | other, | ākhara ]] [[ وَلَا | and do not | wa-lā ]] [[ يَقۡتُلُونَ | kill | yaqtulūna ]] [[ ٱلنَّفۡسَ | the soul | an-nafsa ]] [[ ٱلَّتِي | which | allatī ]] [[ حَرَّمَ | forbade | ḥarrama ]] [[ ٱللَّهُ | Allah, | allāhu ]] [[ إِلَّا | except | illā ]] [[ بِٱلۡحَقِّ | by right, | bil-ḥaqqi ]] [[ وَلَا | and do not | wa-lā ]] [[ يَزۡنُونَۚ | commit zinā. | yaznūna ]] [[ وَمَن | and whoever | wa-man ]] [[ يَفۡعَلۡ | does | yafʿal ]] [[ ذَٰلِكَ | that | dhālika ]] [[ يَلۡقَ | will meet | yalqa ]] [[ أَثَامٗا | a penalty. | athāmā ]] [[۝٦٨]]

**Tick when done**
- [ ] Call on Allah alone
- [ ] Protect every life Allah has made sacred
- [ ] Guard yourself from zinā

## الضَّمَان ۞ Allah's Guarantee

*The repercussion for crossing the lines.* The condition sits in the last clause of 25:68 above, and the outcome continues in the next ayah.

### Ayah 69


Their punishment will be multiplied on the Day of Judgment, and they will remain in it forever, in disgrace. (25:69)

[[ يُضَٰعَفۡ | will be doubled | yuḍāʿaf ]] [[ لَهُ | for him | lahu ]] [[ ٱلۡعَذَابُ | the punishment | al-ʿadhābu ]] [[ يَوۡمَ | on the Day | yawma ]] [[ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ | of Resurrection | al-qiyāmati ]] [[ وَيَخۡلُدۡ | and he will abide | wa-yakhlud ]] [[ فِيهِۦ | in it | fīhi ]] [[ مُهَانًا | humiliated. | muhānā ]] [[۝٦٩]]

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Commentary.** Attributed to Tafsīr al-Qur'ān al-ʿAẓīm, Ibn Kathīr. Verify the wording against the original before citing.

**The three lines are the three greatest wrongs.** Ibn Kathīr records, through Imam Aḥmad, that ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd ؓ asked the Prophet ﷺ which sin is most serious. The answer: appointing a rival to Allah who created you; then killing your child for fear he will eat with you; then committing adultery with your neighbour's wife. ʿAbdullāh said this verse was revealed to confirm it. Ibn Kathīr notes it was also recorded by an-Nasāʾī, al-Bukhārī and Muslim.

**What [[ أَثَامٗا | a penalty | athāmā ]] means.** Ibn Kathīr reports that ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAmr ؓ said Athām is a valley in Hell, and that ʿIkrimah, Saʿīd ibn Jubayr and Mujāhid spoke of valleys in Hell. As-Suddī said it means punishment, which Ibn Kathīr says is closer to the apparent meaning of the verse. On the doubling, he says it is repeated and intensified, and that the disgrace is scorn and humiliation.

</details>

## Step 7 · Return to Allah in Tawbah

[[ إِلَّا | except | illā ]] [[ مَن | one who | man ]] [[ تَابَ | repents | tāba ]] [[ وَءَامَنَ | and believes | wa-āmana ]] [[ وَعَمِلَ | and does | wa-ʿamila ]] [[ عَمَلٗا | deeds | ʿamalan ]] [[ صَٰلِحٗا | righteous, | ṣāliḥan ]] [[ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ | for those | fa-ulāʾika ]] [[ يُبَدِّلُ | will replace | yubaddilu ]] [[ ٱللَّهُ | Allah | allāhu ]] [[ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمۡ | their evil deeds | sayyiʾātihim ]] [[ حَسَنَٰتٖۗ | with good ones. | ḥasanātin ]] [[ وَكَانَ | And is | wa-kāna ]] [[ ٱللَّهُ | Allah | allāhu ]] [[ غَفُورٗا | Oft-Forgiving, | ghafūran ]] [[ رَّحِيمٗا | Most Merciful. | raḥīmā ]] [[۝٧٠]]

### Ayah 71

And whoever repents and does good has truly turned to Allah properly. (25:71)

[[ وَمَن | and whoever | wa-man ]] [[ تَابَ | repents | tāba ]] [[ وَعَمِلَ | and does | wa-ʿamila ]] [[ صَٰلِحٗا | righteous deeds | ṣāliḥan ]] [[ فَإِنَّهُۥ | then indeed he | fa-innahu ]] [[ يَتُوبُ | turns | yatūbu ]] [[ إِلَى | to | ilā ]] [[ ٱللَّهِ | Allah | allāhi ]] [[ مَتَابٗا | in true repentance. | matābā ]] [[۝٧١]]

**Tick when done**
- [ ] Repent to Allah in this life, from every wrong
- [ ] Believe, and keep your faith in what Allah has revealed
- [ ] Follow tawbah with righteous action

## الضَّمَان ۞ Allah's Guarantee

*The reward for the one who returns.* Allah replaces their evil deeds with good ones (25:70), and the one who repents and does good has turned to Allah in true repentance (25:71).

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Commentary.** Attributed to Tafsīr al-Qur'ān al-ʿAẓīm, Ibn Kathīr. Verify the wording against the original before citing.

**The door is open for every sin.** Ibn Kathīr says Allah's mercy extends to all His creation, and that whoever repents, He accepts the repentance for any sin, great or small. He cites 4:110, 9:104 and 39:53. He states that the repentance of the murderer is acceptable, and points to the authentic story of the man who killed one hundred and then repented.

**The last man out of the Fire.** Ibn Kathīr gives the ḥadīth of Abū Dharr ؓ, recorded by Muslim. A man is asked about his minor sins first, admits them all, and is told: for every evil deed you now have one good deed. He answers: "O Lord, I did things that I do not see here." Abū Dharr said the Prophet ﷺ smiled so broadly that his molars showed.

**Even your betrayals.** An old man told the Prophet ﷺ there was no evil he had not done. After he affirmed his faith, the Prophet ﷺ said Allah would forgive him and replace his evil deeds with good ones. The man asked about his betrayals and immoral deeds, and the Prophet ﷺ answered: [[ وَغَدَرَاتُكَ | even your betrayals | wa-ghadarātuka ]] [[ وَفَجَرَاتُكَ | and your immoral deeds. | wa-fajarātuka ]] The man left saying lā ilāha illā Allāh and Allāhu akbar. The road into the ranks of ʿIbād ar-Raḥmān begins with tawbah, not with a perfect past.

</details>

## Step 8 · Stay Away from Falsehood

### Ayah 72

˹They are˺ those who do not bear false witness, and when they come across falsehood, they pass ˹it˺ by with dignity. (25:72)

[[ وَٱلَّذِينَ | and those who | wa-alladhīna ]] [[ لَا | do not | lā ]] [[ يَشۡهَدُونَ | bear witness to | yashhadūna ]] [[ ٱلزُّورَ | falsehood, | az-zūra ]] [[ وَإِذَا | and when | wa-idhā ]] [[ مَرُّواْ | they pass | marrū ]] [[ بِٱللَّغۡوِ | by vain talk, | bil-laghwi ]] [[ مَرُّواْ | they pass | marrū ]] [[ كِرَامٗا | with dignity. | kirāmā ]] [[۝٧٢]]

**Tick when done**
- [ ] Give no false testimony
- [ ] Do not attend places where falsehood is spoken or done
- [ ] Pass vain talk by, and let it leave no mark on you

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Commentary.** Attributed to Tafsīr al-Qur'ān al-ʿAẓīm, Ibn Kathīr. Verify the wording against the original before citing.

**Falsehood has a wide meaning.** Ibn Kathīr includes lies, immorality, disbelief, foul speech and false words. He reports ʿAmr ibn Qays saying it refers to gatherings of sexual immorality, and that some said it refers to false testimony.

**The weight the Prophet ﷺ gave it.** Ibn Kathīr cites the ḥadīth of Abū Bakrah ؓ from the two Ṣaḥīḥs. After naming shirk and disobedience to parents among the greatest major sins, the Prophet ﷺ sat up and warned against false speech and false witness. He kept repeating it until the Companions wished he would stop.

**Absence is the first dignity.** From the context, Ibn Kathīr understands the verse to mean they do not attend falsehood or are not present when it happens. If they do pass by it, they do not let it contaminate them in the slightest.

</details>

## Step 9 · Stay Alive to the Verses of Your Lord

### Ayah 73

˹They are˺ those who, when reminded of the revelation of their Lord, do not turn a blind eye or a deaf ear to it. (25:73)

[[ وَٱلَّذِينَ | and those who | wa-alladhīna ]] [[ إِذَا | when | idhā ]] [[ ذُكِّرُواْ | they are reminded | dhukkirū ]] [[ بِـَٔايَٰتِ | of the verses | biʾāyāti ]] [[ رَبِّهِمۡ | of their Lord, | rabbihim ]] [[ لَمۡ | do not | lam ]] [[ يَخِرُّواْ | fall | yakhirrū ]] [[ عَلَيۡهَا | upon them | ʿalayhā ]] [[ صُمّٗا | deaf | summan ]] [[ وَعُمۡيَانٗا | and blind. | wa-ʿumyānā ]] [[۝٧٣]]

**Tick when done**
- [ ] Listen when the Qur'an is recited
- [ ] Let a reminder change what you do
- [ ] Let the verses grow your faith

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Commentary.** Attributed to Tafsīr al-Qur'ān al-ʿAẓīm, Ibn Kathīr. Verify the wording against the original before citing.

**Hearing is not the same as being moved.** Ibn Kathīr calls this a characteristic of the believers and cites 8:2: when Allah is mentioned their hearts tremble, and when His verses are recited their faith increases. He contrasts the disbelievers, who are not affected or moved to change, and persist as if deaf and blind. He cites 9:124-125: the verses increase the believers in faith and the diseased hearts in doubt.

</details>

## Step 10 · Recite This Dua: Ask for a Family Who Are the Comfort of Your Eyes

### Ayah 74

˹They are˺ those who pray, *"Our Lord! Bless us with ˹pious˺ spouses and offspring who will be the joy of our hearts, and make us models for the righteous."* (25:74)

[[ وَٱلَّذِينَ | and those who | wa-alladhīna ]] [[ يَقُولُونَ | say, | yaqūlūna ]] [[ رَبَّنَا | Our Lord, | rabbanā ]] [[ هَبۡ | grant | hab ]] [[ لَنَا | us | lanā ]] [[ مِنۡ | from | min ]] [[ أَزۡوَٰجِنَا | our spouses | azwājinā ]] [[ وَذُرِّيَّٰتِنَا | and our offspring | wa-dhurriyyātinā ]] [[ قُرَّةَ | the comfort | qurrata ]] [[ أَعۡيُنٖ | of eyes, | aʿyunin ]] [[ وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا | and make us | wa-jʿalnā ]] [[ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ | for the God-conscious | lil-muttaqīna ]] [[ إِمَامًا | a leader. | imāmā ]] [[۝٧٤]]

**Tick when done**
- [ ] Learn the dua by heart
- [ ] Make it for your spouse and your children by name
- [ ] Live as the example you are asking Allah to make you

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Commentary.** Attributed to Tafsīr al-Qur'ān al-ʿAẓīm, Ibn Kathīr. Verify the wording against the original before citing.

**Comfort of the eyes is faith, not appearance.** Ibn Kathīr explains the request as offspring who will obey Allah, worship Him, and associate nothing with Him. He reports Ibn ʿAbbās: offspring who strive to obey Allah and bring joy in this world and the Hereafter. The dua does not ask for a family that looks successful. It asks for a family that stands with you before Allah.

**The ache behind the dua.** Ibn Kathīr records, through Imam Aḥmad, the words of al-Miqdād ibn al-Aswad ؓ, and notes the chain is ṣaḥīḥ. A man could come to know that his father, son or brother was a disbeliever, and knowing where that led, could not rest. Al-Miqdād said this is what the verse refers to. The servant who makes this dua carries his family's ākhirah as his own concern.

**The servant asks to lead, not only to be saved.** On [[ وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا | and make us | wa-jʿalnā ]] [[ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ | for the God-conscious | lil-muttaqīna ]] [[ إِمَامًا | a leader | imāmā ]], Ibn Kathīr reports Ibn ʿAbbās, al-Ḥasan, as-Suddī, Qatādah and Rabīʿ ibn Anas: leaders taken as examples in good. Others said guides who call others to goodness. Ibn Kathīr adds that they wanted their worship joined to the worship of their children, and their guidance to reach beyond themselves. He ties this to the ḥadīth in Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim from Abū Hurayrah ؓ: when a son of Adam dies his deeds cease except three, the first being [[ وَلَدٍ | a child | waladin ]] [[ صَالِحٍ | righteous | ṣāliḥin ]] [[ يَدْعُو | who prays | yadʿū ]] [[ لَهُ | for him. | lahu ]] This dua is an investment that outlives the one who makes it.

</details>

## ✵ The Reward of ʿIbād ar-Raḥmān

## الْوَعْد ✵ Allah's Promise

### Ayah 75

It is they who will be rewarded with ˹elevated˺ mansions ˹in Paradise˺ for their perseverance, and will be received with salutations and ˹greetings of˺ peace, (25:75)

[[ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ | Those | ulāʾika ]] [[ يُجۡزَوۡنَ | will be rewarded | yujzawna ]] [[ ٱلۡغُرۡفَةَ | with the highest place | al-ghurfata ]] [[ بِمَا | because | bimā ]] [[ صَبَرُواْ | they were patient, | ṣabarū ]] [[ وَيُلَقَّوۡنَ | and they will be met | wa-yulaqqawna ]] [[ فِيهَا | therein | fīhā ]] [[ تَحِيَّةٗ | with greeting | taḥiyyatan ]] [[ وَسَلَٰمًا | and peace. | wa-salāmā ]] [[۝٧٥]]

### Ayah 76

staying there forever. What an excellent place to settle and reside! (25:76)

[[ خَٰلِدِينَ | Abiding forever | khālidīna ]] [[ فِيهَاۚ | therein. | fīhā ]] [[ حَسُنَتۡ | Excellent it is | ḥasunat ]] [[ مُسۡتَقَرّٗا | as a settlement | mustaqarran ]] [[ وَمُقَامٗا | and residence. | wa-muqāmā ]] [[۝٧٦]]

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Commentary.** Attributed to Tafsīr al-Qur'ān al-ʿAẓīm, Ibn Kathīr. Verify the wording against the original before citing.

**The reward names the price.** The Promise gives one reason: [[ بِمَا | because | bimā ]] [[ صَبَرُواْ | they were patient. | ṣabarū ]] Ibn Kathīr reads it as their patience in doing what they did, everything the passage described. Every trait of ʿIbād ar-Raḥmān costs patience.

**The highest place.** Ibn Kathīr says [[ ٱلۡغُرۡفَةَ | the highest place | al-ghurfata ]] is Paradise, and reports Abū Jaʿfar al-Bāqir, Saʿīd ibn Jubayr, aḍ-Ḍaḥḥāk and as-Suddī saying it is so called because of its elevation.

**The welcome.** They are greeted first with words of welcome and honor. Peace is theirs and they are wished peace. Angels enter to them from every gate, saying peace be upon you for what you endured in patience. The ones who answered the ignorant with [[ سَلَٰمٗا | peace | salāmā ]] are answered in Paradise with [[ سَلَٰمًا | peace. | salāmā ]]

**No leaving.** Ibn Kathīr explains [[ خَٰلِدِينَ | abiding | khālidīna ]]: they settle there and never leave, move or die, and never wish to be anywhere else. He compares 11:108. He glosses the last line as beautiful in appearance and good to rest and dwell in.

</details>

## الضَّمَان ۞ Allah's Guarantee

*Addressed to the deniers.* The second half of 25:77 states the consequence of denial: the punishment will be inevitable.

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Commentary.** Attributed to Tafsīr al-Qur'ān al-ʿAẓīm, Ibn Kathīr. Verify the wording against the original before citing.

**Worship is the reason for the attention.** Ibn Kathīr says Allah would not care to pay attention to you if you did not worship Him, for He created mankind only to worship Him alone and glorify Him morning and evening.

**What the denial leads to.** He reads [[ لِزَامَۢا | the inevitable punishment | lizāmā ]] as your denial remaining with you, leading to punishment, doom and destruction in this world and the Hereafter. Ibn Masʿūd, Ubayy ibn Kaʿb, Muḥammad ibn Kaʿb al-Quraẓī, Mujāhid, aḍ-Ḍaḥḥāk, Qatādah, as-Suddī and others took it as the day of Badr. al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī said the Day of Resurrection. Ibn Kathīr says there is no conflict between the two.

</details>
