# Du'a Tolak Balak - Calamity Averted Before It Descends

A supplication from the Ba'Alawī tradition of Ḥaḍramawt, recited on **Tuesday**. Recite the whole supplication **three times**, then *Yā Allāh* **seven times**.
> *"O Allah, O Sufficer against calamity, suffice us against calamity before it descends from the heaven."*

## 📜✨ Du'a

[[ x 3 ]][[ اللَّهُمَّ | O Allah | Allāhumma ]] [[ يَا | O | yā ]] [[ كَافِيَ | Sufficer | kāfiya ]] [[ الْبَلَاءِ | against calamity | l-balāʾi ]]

[[ اكْفِنَا | suffice us | ikfinā ]] [[ الْبَلَاءَ | against calamity | l-balāʾa ]] 

[[ قَبْلَ | before | qabla ]] [[ نُزُولِهِ | its descent | nuzūlihi ]] [[ مِنَ | from | mina ]] [[ السَّمَاءِ | the heaven | s-samāʾi ]]

[[ x 7 ]][[ يَا | O | yā ]] [[ اللَّهُ | Allah | Allāh ]]

## {{ Hikmah | @hikmah }}

Whoever recites it three times on Tuesday is protected: no calamity will descend on them until the following Tuesday.
