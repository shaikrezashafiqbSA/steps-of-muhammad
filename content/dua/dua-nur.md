# The Du'a of Light upon Waking at Night

Ibn ʿAbbās ؓ spent a night at the house of Maymūnah. **The Prophet ﷺ woke in the night**, answered the call of nature, washed his face and hands, and slept again. He rose, performed wuḍūʾ from a water skin, and prayed thirteen rakʿah, with Ibn ʿAbbās standing beside him.

Afterwards he lay down and slept until Bilāl informed him of the Fajr prayer, and he prayed without renewing wuḍūʾ. **He used to say in his invocation:**
> *"O Allah, place light in my heart, light in my sight, light in my hearing, light on my right, light on my left, light above me, light below me, light in front of me, light behind me, and make for me light."*

### Further words reported by Kurayb

Kurayb said he had forgotten seven other words. A man from the offspring of al-ʿAbbās narrated them to him, mentioning (light in):

[[ عَصَبِي | my nerves | ʿaṣabī ]] [[ وَلَحْمِي | and my flesh | wa-laḥmī ]] [[ وَدَمِي | and my blood | wa-damī ]] [[ وَشَعَرِي | and my hair | wa-shaʿarī ]] [[ وَبَشَرِي | and my body | wa-basharī ]]
