# Verification Notice (Read First)

**This text is a composite du'ā, not a single narrated hadith.** It has no isnad and cannot be assigned one grade. Four corrections apply before you use the formatted version below:

1. **The birthday opening is not from the Sunnah.** "Sanah ḥalwah ḥabībī" is a modern colloquial Arabic birthday greeting. It carries no prophetic attribution. I have separated it from the narrated material below.
2. **The "Aghninā biḥalālika" wording differs from the authenticated text.** The verified narration is *"Allāhumma-kfinī biḥalālika ʿan ḥarāmik, wa aghninī bifaḍlika ʿamman siwāk"* — Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 3563 (Riyāḍ aṣ-Ṣāliḥīn 1486), narrated by ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭālib (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu), graded ḥasan. Your source attributes it to Ibn as-Sunnī's *ʿAmal al-Yawm wa al-Layla*. **I could not verify that attribution.**
3. **The clause "wa biṭāʿatika ʿan maʿṣiyatik" has no verified chain.** I searched and found it only on social media and du'ā-compilation sites, never with a takhrīj. Treat it as a permissible personal addition, not a transmitted Sunnah wording.
4. **"Yā Ghaniyyu yā Ḥamīd, yā Mubdi'u yā Muʿīd, yā Raḥīmu yā Wadūd" has no verified hadith source.** I could not confirm the "Bukhārī 2736" citation in your document as applying to this specific sequence. Calling on Allah by His names is established by Qur'an 7:180, but this particular formula is not a transmitted prophetic du'ā.

I have marked each section's status inline. Only sections 3, 4, and 6 below carry verified prophetic authority.

# Du'ā — Yā Ghaniyyu Aghninā
### Supplication for Sufficiency, Repentance, and the Good of Both Worlds

## 📜 The Supplications

**On the du'ā of independence from the unlawful (Tirmidhī 3563 — Ḥasan):** ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭālib (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu) reported that a mukātab (a slave under a contract of emancipation) came to him and said he could not meet his payment. ʿAlī said: "Shall I not teach you words the Messenger of Allah ﷺ taught me? Even if you owed a debt the size of Mount Ṣīr, Allah would settle it for you. Say: *Allāhumma-kfinī biḥalālika ʿan ḥarāmik, wa aghninī bifaḍlika ʿamman siwāk.*"

**On the du'ā of Dhun-Nūn (Tirmidhī 3505 — Ṣaḥīḥ):** Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu) reported that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The supplication of Dhun-Nūn when he called out from inside the whale was: *Lā ilāha illā anta subḥānaka innī kuntu minaẓ-ẓālimīn.* No Muslim ever supplicates with it for anything except that Allah answers him."

**On the comprehensive du'ā (Qur'an 2:201):** Allah instructs the believers to say: *Rabbanā ātinā fid-dunyā ḥasanah wa fil-ākhirati ḥasanah wa qinā ʿadhāban-nār.* Al-Bukhārī and Muslim both record that this was the supplication the Prophet ﷺ made most frequently. I have not verified the exact hadith numbers for those two reports.

## 🔤 Word-by-Word

### ⚠️ Section 1 — Opening Greeting (NOT from the Sunnah)

This is a cultural Arabic greeting. It carries no prophetic attribution and no narrated reward. It is permissible speech, not dhikr.

[[ سَنَةٌ | a year | sanah ]] [[ حَلْوَةٌ | sweet | ḥalwah ]] [[ حَبِيبِي | my beloved | ḥabībī ]] [[ وَيَا أُسْتَاذِي | and O my teacher | wa yā ustādhī ]]

[[ بَارَكَ | may He bless | bāraka ]] [[ اللَّهُ | Allah | Allāhu ]] [[ فِي عُمْرِكُمْ | in your lifespan | fī ʿumrikum ]] [[ وَحَيَاتِكُمْ | and your life | wa ḥayātikum ]]

*Note: "Bārakallāhu fī ʿumrik" is a valid general du'ā in form. It is not a transmitted prophetic formula tied to birthdays.*

---

### ⚠️ Section 2 — Calling on the Divine Names (source unverified)

Qur'an 7:180 establishes calling on Allah by His beautiful names. This specific six-name sequence is not traceable to a verified narration.

[[ اللَّهُمَّ | O Allah | Allāhumma ]] [[ يَا غَنِيُّ | O Self-Sufficient | yā Ghaniyy ]] [[ يَا حَمِيدُ | O Praiseworthy | yā Ḥamīd ]]

[[ يَا مُبْدِئُ | O Originator | yā Mubdi' ]] [[ يَا مُعِيدُ | O Restorer | yā Muʿīd ]]

[[ يَا رَحِيمُ | O Most Merciful | yā Raḥīm ]] [[ يَا وَدُودُ | O Most Loving | yā Wadūd ]]

### ✅ Section 3 — Sufficiency by the Lawful (Tirmidhī 3563 — Ḥasan)

**Authenticated wording:**

[[ اللَّهُمَّ اكْفِنِي | O Allah, suffice me | Allāhumma-kfinī ]] [[ بِحَلَالِكَ | with Your lawful provision | biḥalālik ]] [[ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ | over Your unlawful | ʿan ḥarāmik ]]

[[ وَأَغْنِنِي | and enrich me | wa aghninī ]] [[ بِفَضْلِكَ | by Your bounty | bifaḍlik ]] [[ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ | over anyone besides You | ʿamman siwāk ]]

**The Divine Guarantee**

[[ لَوْ كَانَ عَلَيْكَ | if you had upon you | law kāna ʿalayka ]] [[ مِثْلُ جَبَلِ صِيرٍ | the like of Mount Ṣīr | mithlu jabali Ṣīr ]] [[ دَيْنًا | in debt | daynan ]] [[ أَدَّاهُ اللَّهُ عَنْكَ | Allah would settle it for you | addāhu-llāhu ʿanka ]]

**⚠️ Unverified additional clause** (present in your source, no chain found):

[[ وَبِطَاعَتِكَ عَنْ مَعْصِيَتِكَ | and with obedience to You over disobedience to You | wa biṭāʿatika ʿan maʿṣiyatik ]]

### ✅ Section 4 — The Prayer of Dhun-Nūn (Qur'an 21:87 · Tirmidhī 3505 — Ṣaḥīḥ)

[[ لَا إِلَٰهَ | there is no god | lā ilāha ]] [[ إِلَّا أَنْتَ | except You | illā anta ]] [[ سُبْحَانَكَ | exalted are You | subḥānaka ]]

[[ إِنِّي كُنْتُ | indeed I have been | innī kuntu ]] [[ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ | among the wrongdoers | minaẓ-ẓālimīn ]]

**The Divine Guarantee**

[[ فَإِنَّهُ لَمْ يَدْعُ بِهَا | for no one supplicates with it | fa-innahu lam yadʿu bihā ]] [[ رَجُلٌ مُسْلِمٌ | a Muslim man | rajulun muslim ]] [[ فِي شَيْءٍ قَطُّ | for anything, ever | fī shay'in qaṭṭ ]] [[ إِلَّا اسْتَجَابَ اللَّهُ لَهُ | except that Allah answers him | illa-stajāba-llāhu lah ]]

### ⚠️ Section 5 — Petition for Provision (composed du'ā, no chain)

The names invoked are established. The composed sequence is not a transmitted narration. It remains permissible as personal supplication.

[[ أَغْنِنَا | enrich us | aghninā ]] [[ يَا غَنِيُّ | O Self-Sufficient | yā Ghaniyy ]]

[[ أَغْنِنَا | enrich us | aghninā ]] [[ يَا مُغْنِي | O Enricher | yā Mughnī ]]

[[ وَارْزُقْنَا | and provide for us | warzuqnā ]] [[ يَا رَزَّاقُ | O Provider | yā Razzāq ]]

[[ وَوَسِّعْ | and expand | wa wassiʿ ]] [[ أَرْزَاقَنَا | our provisions | arzāqanā ]]

[[ بِرَحْمَتِكَ | by Your mercy | biraḥmatik ]] [[ يَا أَكْرَمَ الْأَكْرَمِينَ | O Most Generous of the generous | yā Akramal-Akramīn ]]

[[ وَيَا أَرْحَمَ الرَّاحِمِينَ | and O Most Merciful of the merciful | wa yā Arḥamar-Rāḥimīn ]]

[[ وَيَا رَزَّاقَ | and O Provider, | wa yā Razzāqa ]] [[ ذِي الْقُوَّةِ الْمَتِينِ | Owner of firm strength | dhil-quwwatil-matīn ]]

*"Ar-Razzāq Dhul-Quwwatil-Matīn" derives from Qur'an 51:58.*

### ✅ Section 6 — The Comprehensive Du'ā (Qur'an 2:201)

[[ رَبَّنَا | our Lord | Rabbanā ]] [[ آتِنَا | grant us | ātinā ]] [[ فِي الدُّنْيَا | in this world | fid-dunyā ]] [[ حَسَنَةً | good | ḥasanah ]]

### ✅ Section 7 — The Seal (Ṣalawāt)

[[ وَصَلَّى اللَّهُ | and may Allah send prayers | wa ṣallallāhu ]] [[ عَلَىٰ سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ | upon our master Muhammad | ʿalā sayyidinā Muḥammad ]]

[[ وَعَلَىٰ آلِهِ | and upon his family | wa ʿalā ālihi ]] [[ وَصَحْبِهِ | and his companions | wa ṣaḥbihi ]] [[ وَسَلَّمَ | and grant peace | wa sallam ]]

*The word "sayyidinā" does not appear in the transmitted wording of the ṣalawāt. Its addition is accepted by the majority of scholars and disputed by some. Both positions exist; verify against your own school's position.*
