# The Dua Allah Praised: Good in Both Worlds and protection from punishment of the Fire

## 📜 The Qur'anic Verse

Of mankind there are some who say: "Our Lord! Give us (Your bounties) in this world!" and for such there will be no portion in the Hereafter. (2:200)

And of them there are some who say: *"Our Lord! Give us in this world that which is good and in the Hereafter that which is good, and save us from the torment of the Fire!"* (2:201)

For them there will be alloted a share for what they have earned. And Allah is swift at reckoning. (2:202)

**Three things to ask for, and three things to carry.** Ibn Kathīr quotes al-Qāsim ibn ʿAbd ar-Raḥmān: whoever is given a grateful heart, a remembering tongue, and a patient body has received good in this life, good in the Hereafter, and safety from the Fire.

**This dua corrected a prayer that stopped halfway.** Ibn Kathīr reports Ibn ʿAbbās, through Saʿīd ibn Jubayr: some bedouins would come to ʿArafah and ask for a rainy year, a fertile year, and a year of good child bearing, with no mention of the Hereafter. Allah revealed of such a person: 

[[ وَمَا | and not | wa-mā ]] [[ لَهُ | for him | lahu ]] [[ فِي | in | fī ]] [[ الْآخِرَةِ | the Hereafter | al-ākhirati ]] [[ مِنْ | any | min ]] [[ خَلَاقٍ | share | khalāqin ]]. 

The believers who came after them said this dua. Ibn Kathīr says the criticism is meant to stop others from imitating that prayer, and that Allah praised those who ask for both this life and the next. The fault is not in asking for this world. It is in asking for it alone.

**It comes after remembrance.** The verse sits after the command to remember Allah once the Ḥajj rites are done. Ibn Kathīr reports Ibn ʿAbbās on the people of Jāhiliyyah: their only dhikr at the season was recounting what their fathers had done. Allah told them to remember Him as they remembered their fathers, or more. Ibn Kathīr adds that Allah encourages supplication after remembrance because it makes acceptance more likely. The order is dhikr first, then the dua.

**One ḥasanah, a wide field.** Ibn Kathīr lists what scholars of tafsīr include in the good of this life: well-being, a spacious dwelling, pleasing spouses, sufficient provision, beneficial knowledge, good work and deeds, comfortable transport, and good praise. He calls all of it only a part. For the Hereafter, the best good is Paradise, which includes safety from the greatest horror at the gathering place, being questioned lightly, and the other favours there. Protection from the Fire reaches back into this world: being directed to what leads away from it, such as avoiding prohibitions, sins of every kind, and doubtful matters.


**The Prophet ﷺ said it, and prescribed it.** Ibn Kathīr cites al-Bukhārī from Anas ؓ that the Prophet ﷺ used to say this dua. He also cites Aḥmad, with Muslim recording it too: the Prophet ﷺ visited a Muslim man weakened to the state of a sick small bird. The man said he used to ask Allah to give him in this life whatever punishment was stored for him in the Hereafter. The Prophet ﷺ said he could not bear it and asked why he had not said this dua instead. The man began to recite it and was cured.

## Variation 1: The Prophet's most frequent Dua

Qatādah asked Anas ؓ which supplication the Prophet ﷺ made most often. Anas said that the supplication he made most often was:

[[ اللَّهُمَّ | O Allah, | allāhumma ]][[ آتِنَا | grant us | ātinā ]] [[ فِي | in | fī ]] [[ الدُّنْيَا | the world | ad-dunyā ]] [[ حَسَنَةً | good, | ḥasanatan ]][[ وَفِي | and in | wa-fī ]] [[ الْآخِرَةِ | the Hereafter | al-ākhirati ]] [[ حَسَنَةً | good, | ḥasanatan ]][[ وَقِنَا | and protect us from | wa-qinā ]] [[ عَذَابَ | the punishment | ʿadhāba ]] [[ النَّارِ | of the Fire. | an-nāri ]]

Qatādah said that whenever Anas wanted to make a supplication, he made this one. And whenever he wanted to make a different supplication, he made this one within it.

## Variation 2: Between the Two Corners: The Dua of Ṭawāf

ʿAbd Allāh ibn as-Sāʾib ؓ said: I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ say, between the two corners:

*"Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire."*
