# The Congregational Duʿāʾ for Parents and All Believers — A Multi-Billion Ḥasanah Investment

## 📜 The Hadith

The Prophet ﷺ said: ["Whoever seeks forgiveness for the believing men and the believing women, Allah will record for him one good deed for every single believing man and believing woman."](https://www.abuaminaelias.com/dailyhadithonline/2013/08/15/istighfar-for-the-believers/)

Building upon this, the imams of the masājid recite the expanded congregational supplication.


## 🔤 Word-by-Word

[[ اللَّهُمَّ | O Allah, | Allāhumma ]][[ اغْفِرْ | forgive | ighfir ]][[ لَنَا | for us | lanā ]][[ ذُنُوبَنَا | our sins, | dhunūbanā ]]

[[ وَلِوَالِدِينَا | and for our parents, | wa-li-wālidaynā ]][[ وَارْحَمْهُمَا | and have mercy on them both, | war-ḥamhumā ]][[ كَمَا | just as | kamā ]][[ رَبَّيَانِي | they raised me | rabbayānī ]][[ صَغِيرًا | when I was small. | ṣaghīrā ]]

[[ وَلِجَمِيعِ | and for all | wa-li-jamīʿi ]][[ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ | the believing men | al-muʾminīna ]][[ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ | and the believing women, | wal-muʾmināti ]]

[[ وَالْمُسْلِمِينَ | and the Muslim men | wal-muslimīna ]][[ وَالْمُسْلِمَاتِ | and the Muslim women, | wal-muslimāti ]]

[[ الْأَحْيَاءِ | the living | al-aḥyāʾi ]][[ مِنْهُمْ | among them | minhum ]][[ وَالْأَمْوَاتِ | and the deceased, | wal-amwāti ]]

[[ بِرَحْمَتِكَ | by Your mercy, | bi-raḥmatika ]][[ يَا | O | yā ]][[ أَرْحَمَ | Most Merciful | arḥama ]][[ الرَّاحِمِينَ | of those who show mercy. | ar-rāḥimīna ]]

## الضَّمَان ۞ Allah's Guarantee

The Prophet ﷺ attached an explicit, quantified consequence to seeking forgiveness for the believers — the condition and its result:

[[ مَنِ | Whoever | mani ]][[ اسْتَغْفَرَ | seeks forgiveness | istaghfara ]][[ لِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ | for the believing men | lil-muʾminīna ]][[ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ | and the believing women, | wal-muʾmināti ]]

[[ كَتَبَ | records | kataba ]][[ اللَّهُ | Allah | Allāhu ]][[ لَهُ | for him | lahu ]][[ بِكُلِّ | for every | bi-kulli ]][[ مُؤْمِنٍ | believing man | muʾminin ]][[ وَمُؤْمِنَةٍ | and believing woman, | wa-muʾminatin ]][[ حَسَنَةً | one good deed. | ḥasanatan ]]

The duʿāʾ is architected as an ever-widening circle of mercy. It begins with the self — `[[ لَنَا | for us | lanā ]]` — expands to the parents — `[[ وَلِوَالِدِينَا | and for our parents | wa-li-wālidaynā ]]` — and finally embraces the entire ummah — `[[ وَلِجَمِيعِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ | and for all the believing men | wa-li-jamīʿi l-muʾminīna ]]`.

The text also binds the living to the dead in one petition: `[[ الْأَحْيَاءِ مِنْهُمْ وَالْأَمْوَاتِ | the living among them and the deceased | al-aḥyāʾi minhum wal-amwāti ]]`. Paired with the guarantee of one ḥasanah per believer, every recitation reaches backward through fourteen centuries and forward to believers yet unborn — a single sentence carrying a reward counted in billions.

Notice too how the duʿāʾ seals itself not with our deserving but with His attribute: `[[ يَا أَرْحَمَ الرَّاحِمِينَ | O Most Merciful of those who show mercy | yā arḥama r-rāḥimīna ]]`. The plea for billions is anchored in the only Mercy vast enough to answer it.
