# The Supplication for Rectification of Religion, World, and Hereafter

## 📜 The Hadith

Abū Hurayrah ؓ reported: The Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to say: *"O Allah, set right for me my religion, which is the safeguard of my affairs; set right for me my world, in which is my livelihood; set right for me my Hereafter, to which is my return; and make life an increase for me in every good, and make death a relief for me from every evil."*

[Muslim]

<details>
<summary>۞ الْحِكْمَة — Divine Wisdom</summary>

> **Source: Textual.** This reading follows from the wording of the text itself.

The supplication is built on three parallel petitions, each tying a realm to its function. Religion is named [[ عِصْمَةُ | the safeguard | ʿiṣmatu ]] [[ أَمْرِي | of my affair | amrī ]] — the thing that protects the whole. The world is reduced to its essence: [[ فِيهَا | in it | fīhā ]] [[ مَعَاشِي | my livelihood | maʿāshī ]]. The Hereafter is framed by its direction: [[ فِيهَا | in it | fīhā ]] [[ مَعَادِي | my return | maʿādī ]].

The text then resolves time itself into two requests. Life is asked for not as length but as [[ زِيَادَةً | an increase | ziyādatan ]] [[ فِي | in | fī ]] [[ كُلِّ | every | kulli ]] [[ خَيْرٍ | good | khayrin ]] — its value is measured by accumulation of good, not years. Death, the feared counterpart, is redefined as [[ رَاحَةً | a relief | rāḥatan ]] [[ مِنْ | from | min ]] [[ كُلِّ | every | kulli ]] [[ شَرٍّ | evil | sharrin ]] — for one whose affairs are rectified, even death is rest, not loss. The wording thus orders the believer's priorities: religion first as protection, the world second as provision, the Hereafter as destination, and both life and death made servants of good.

</details>
