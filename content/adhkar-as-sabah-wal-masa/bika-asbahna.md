# Allāhumma Bika Aṣbaḥnā — By You We Enter the Day

## 📜 The Hadith
Abū Hurayrah (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu) reported that the Prophet ﷺ used to say **in the morning**: *"O Allah, by You we enter the morning, by You we enter the evening, by You we live, by You we die, and to You is the resurrection."* And **in the evening** he would say: *"O Allah, by You we enter the evening, by You we live, by You we die, and to You is the return."*

Graded **Ṣaḥīḥ** by al-Albānī.
