# 33 · 33 · 33 and the Hundredth - Sins forgiven though they be the foam of the sea

Some poor Emigrants came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and said the wealthy had attained the highest ranks and everlasting bliss through their wealth. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Shall I not teach you something by means of which you will catch up with those who have surpassed you and by which you will supersede those who come after you...? 

"Whoever recites these after every prayer — his sins are forgiven even if they were like the foam of the sea."
