Tahlīl al‑Arwāḥ

A Comprehensive Guidebook to Tahlil Arwah – From Origins to Every Word

📜 Part 1 · Origins – Where Does Tahlil Come From?

What Does “Tahlil” Mean? The word tahlīl (تَهْلِيل) comes from the Arabic root h-l-l, literally meaning the act of saying Lā ilāha illa Allāh – “There is no god but Allah.” In the Malay‑Muslim world (Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Brunei), Tahlil Arwah is a communal gathering where the Qurʾān is recited, ṣalawāt are sent upon the Prophet ﷺ, and duʿā is made for the deceased.

The Wali Songo – the Nine Saints of Java (15th–16th centuries) – structured this practice by retaining pre‑Islamic gathering forms (7th, 40th, 100th day) but replacing non‑Islamic content with Qurʾān, dhikr, and prayers. Later, scholars like Imam Sayyid ʿAbdullāh bin ʿAlawī al‑Ḥaddād (d. 1720 CE) formalised the sequence, making tahlil a blend of prophetic teaching, scholarly refinement, and local wisdom.

Is Tahlil Permitted? Yes. Imam An‑Nawawī affirmed that the reward of Qurʾān recitation reaches the deceased through duʿā. The Qurʾān commands praying for those who preceded us in faith (59:10), and the Prophet ﷺ said: “No people sit remembering Allah but that the angels surround them…” (Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2700). Charity on behalf of the deceased is also established (Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 1004).

💡 Part 2 · Rationale – Why Do We Perform Tahlil?

Spiritual benefits: Renewal of faith (tawḥīd), sending reward to the deceased, and following the Sunnah (reciting Sūrah Yāsīn for the dying and the dead).

Psychological & communal: Comfort for the grieving, strengthening family bonds, and educating the young.

Why Sūrah Yāsīn? The Prophet ﷺ said: “Everything has a heart, and the heart of the Qurʾān is Yā Sīn.” (Sunan al‑Tirmidhī) Imām al‑Ghazālī explained that it affirms resurrection and the afterlife – the very realities the deceased have entered.

Part 3 · The Basic Structure of a Tahlil Gathering
📋 Sequence (core steps)
  1. Opening with Sūrah al‑Fātiḥah (dedications to Prophet ﷺ, family, companions)
  2. Shahādah (testimony of faith) – 3 times
  3. First tahlīl sequence – 4 declarations of Tawḥīd
  4. Second tahlīl sequence – 9 praises of Allah
  5. Istighfār (seeking forgiveness) including Sayyid al‑Istighfār
  6. Dedication of al‑Fātiḥah to the Prophet, saints, teachers, and all deceased
  7. Ṣalawāt – Yā Rabbī ṣalli ʿalā Muḥammad (23 verses)
  8. Qurʾānic prelude (Sūrah al‑Fatḥ 48:1‑3 & Sūrah al‑Tawbah 9:128‑129)
  9. Recitation of Sūrah Yāsīn (entire chapter 36)
  10. Closing ṣalawāt: al‑Mubīn, Nāriyyah, Bushrā
  11. Final duʿā for the deceased and living, Sūrah al‑ʿAṣr, and Tasbīḥ Kaffārah

📖 About This Recitation

This is the complete tahlil arwah as traditionally recited in Southeast Asian Muslim communities. Every line is presented with Arabic text (fully vowelled), romanisation for pronunciation, and English translation. Sections follow the customary order: opening al‑Fātiḥah and shahādah, four tawḥīd declarations, nine divine praises, istighfār (including Sayyid al‑Istighfār), dedications to the Prophet ﷺ, saints, teachers, and all deceased, the 23‑verse ṣalawāt (Yā Rabbī ṣalli ʿalā Muḥammad), the Qurʾānic prelude, and closing supplications.

أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ، بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِI seek refuge...Aʿūdhu billāhi mina al‑shayṭāni al‑rajīm, bismillāhi al‑raḥmāni al‑raḥīm
Indeed, We have granted you a clear triumph – so that Allah may forgive what came before of your sins and what will come after – and complete His favour upon you, and guide you to a straight path. And Allah will support you with a mighty victory. (48:1‑3)
إِنَّا فَتَحْنَا لَكَ فَتْحًا مُبِينًا، لِيَغْفِرَ لَكَ اللَّهُ مَا تَقَدَّمَ مِنْ ذَنْبِكَ وَمَا تَأَخَّرَ، وَيُتِمَّ نِعْمَتَهُ عَلَيْكَ وَيَهْدِيَكَ صِرَاطًا مُسْتَقِيمًا، وَيَنْصُرَكَ اللَّهُ نَصْرًا عَزِيزًاWe have granted you a clear triumphInnā fataḥnā laka fatḥan mubīnā...
There has certainly come to you a Messenger from among yourselves – it grieves him that you should suffer, he is deeply concerned for you, and to the believers he is full of kindness and mercy. But if they turn away, say: “Sufficient for me is Allah. There is no god but Him. In Him I have placed my trust, and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne.” (9:128‑129)
لَقَدْ جَاءَكُمْ رَسُولٌ مِنْ أَنْفُسِكُمْ عَزِيزٌ عَلَيْهِ مَا عَنِتُّمْ حَرِيصٌ عَلَيْكُمْ بِالْمُؤْمِنِينَ رَءُوفٌ رَحِيمٌ، فَإِنْ تَوَلَّوْا فَقُلْ حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَهُوَ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِThere has come to you a Messenger...Laqad jāʾakum rasūlun min anfusikum...
Section 8 · Sūrah Yāsīn – The Heart of the Tahlil
📖 Note

The entire Sūrah Yāsīn (chapter 36 of the Qurʾān) is recited at this point. Its 83 verses affirm tawḥīd, prophethood, resurrection, and Allah’s absolute power. After its completion, the gathering continues with the closing ṣalawāt and duʿā.

Section 9 · Closing Ṣalawāt – al‑Mubīn, Nāriyyah, Bushrā
Ṣalawāt al‑Mubīn (recited 3 or 11 times):
May our Lord Allah send blessings upon the Clear Light, Aḥmad the Chosen One, the master of the messengers.
صَلَّى اللَّهُ رَبُّنَا عَلَى النُّورِ الْمُبِينِ، أَحْمَدَ الْمُصْطَفَى، سَيِّدِ الْمُرْسَلِينَMay Allah bless the Clear Light, Aḥmad al‑MuṣṭafāṢallallāhu rabbunā ʿalā al‑nūri al‑mubīn, Aḥmada al‑muṣṭafā, sayyidi al‑mursalīn
Ṣalawāt Nāriyyah (once):
“O Allah, send a complete ṣalawāt and perfect peace upon our master Muḥammad – through whom knots are untied, difficulties are removed, needs are fulfilled, desires are attained and good endings are secured, and through whose noble face rain is sought – and upon his family and companions, in every moment and breath, to the number of all that is known to You.”
اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ صَلَاةً كَامِلَةً وَسَلِّمْ سَلَامًا تَامًّا عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ، الَّذِي تَنْحَلُّ بِهِ الْعُقَدُ وَتَنْفَرِجُ بِهِ الْكُرَبُ وَتُقْضَى بِهِ الْحَوَائِجُ وَتُنَالُ بِهِ الرَّغَائِبُ وَحُسْنُ الْخَوَاتِمِ وَيُسْتَسْقَى الْغَمَامُ بِوَجْهِهِ الْكَرِيمِ، وَعَلَى آلِهِ وَصَحْبِهِ فِي كُلِّ لَمْحَةٍ وَنَفَسٍ بِعَدَدِ كُلِّ مَعْلُومٍ لَكَO Allah, send complete blessings...Allāhumma ṣalli ṣalātan kāmilatan...
Ṣalawāt Bushrā (once):
“O Allah, send prayers and peace upon our master Muḥammad, the bearer of glad tidings – a ṣalawāt through which You give us glad tidings, and our families, our children, all our teachers, and our male and female students, from this day until the Day of the Hereafter.”
اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ صَاحِبِ الْبُشْرَى، صَلَاةً تُبَشِّرُنَا بِهَا وَأَهْلَنَا وَأَوْلَادَنَا وَجَمِيعَ مَشَايِخِنَا وَمُعَلِّمِينَا وَطَلَبَتِنَا وَطَالِبَاتِنَا، مِنْ يَوْمِنَا هَذَا إِلَى يَوْمِ الْآخِرَةِO Allah, bless Muḥammad the bearer of glad tidings...Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim ʿalā sayyidinā Muḥammad ṣāḥibi al‑bushrā...
Section 10 · Final Duʿā, Sūrah al‑ʿAṣr, and Tasbīḥ Kaffārah
Sūrah al‑ʿAṣr (103):
By time – indeed, mankind is at a loss – except for those who believe, do righteous deeds, counsel one another to truth, and counsel one another to patience.
وَالْعَصْرِ، إِنَّ الْإِنْسَانَ لَفِي خُسْرٍ، إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ، وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالْحَقِّ، وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالصَّبْرِBy time...Wal‑ʿaṣr, inna al‑insāna lafī khusr...
Duʿā for the Qurʾān:
“O Allah, have mercy on us through the Qurʾān. Make it for us a leader, a light, a guidance, and a mercy. O Allah, remind us of what we have forgotten from it, teach us what we do not know of it, grant us the ability to recite it in the hours of the night and at the edges of the day, and make it an argument in our favour, O Lord of all worlds.”
اللَّهُمَّ ارْحَمْنَا بِالْقُرْآنِ وَاجْعَلْهُ لَنَا إِمَامًا وَنُورًا وَهُدًى وَرَحْمَةً، اللَّهُمَّ ذَكِّرْنَا مِنْهُ مَا نُسِّينَا وَعَلِّمْنَا مِنْهُ مَا جَهِلْنَا، وَارْزُقْنَا تِلَاوَتَهُ آنَاءَ اللَّيْلِ وَأَطْرَافَ النَّهَارِ، وَاجْعَلْهُ لَنَا حُجَّةً يَا رَبَّ الْعَالَمِينَO Allah, have mercy on us through the Qurʾān...Allāhumma rḥamnā bil‑Qurʾān...
Tasbīḥ Kaffārah (Expiation of the Gathering):
“Glory be to You, O Allah, and by Your praise. I bear witness that there is no god but You. I seek Your forgiveness and I turn to You in repentance.”
سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، أَسْتَغْفِرُكَ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْكَGlory and praise be to You...Subḥānaka Allāhumma wa biḥamdika, ashhadu an lā ilāha illā ant, astaghfiruka wa atūbu ilayk
Ḥasan Sunan al‑Tirmidhī 3433
🤲 Completion

Thus the tahlil recitation is completed. May Allah accept it from us, forgive our deceased, and unite us all in His mercy. Āmīn.
