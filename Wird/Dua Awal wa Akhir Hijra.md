# Doa Akhir Tahun · End of Year Supplication
### Recited before Maghrib on the 29th of Zulhijjah, seeking forgiveness for the deeds of the past year

> **How to use:** Recite this du'ā 3 times before the Maghrib adhān on the last day of Zulhijjah. It is a moment of deep reflection, repentance for shortcomings, and hopeful anticipation of Allah's acceptance.

---

> **Full meaning:** *In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful. All praise is for Allah, Lord of all the worlds. Prayers and peace be upon our master Muḥammad, and upon his family and all his companions.*

---
## Section 2 · Supplication for Forgiveness & Acceptance

**Instruction:** *Do'a memohon ampun atas dosa-dosa yang terlupa dan diterimanya amal baik* — Supplication seeking forgiveness for sins we have forgotten and acceptance of good deeds.

|---|---|---|

---

> **Full meaning:** *O Allah, whatever we have done in this year from the things You have forbidden and are not pleased with, yet we have not repented from them, and we have forgotten them but You have not forgotten them — and still You are forbearing toward us despite Your power to punish us, and You call us to repentance after our audacity in sinning against You — we seek Your forgiveness, so forgive us. And whatever we have done that pleases You, for which You promised reward and forgiveness, we ask You, O Allah, to accept it from us and not cut off our hope in You. Grant us the reward of the patient for all the worries and trials that have befallen us, O Most Generous, O Most Merciful of the merciful.*

## Section 3 · Supplication for Safety, Climate, and Protection

**Instruction:** *Do'a memohon keamanan, perlindungan dari fitnah dan perubahan iklim* — Supplication for safety, protection from trials, and from harmful climate changes.

---

---

> **Full meaning:** *O Allah, we ask You for safety, security, peace, and well‑being in the whole world and for all of humanity. Keep away from us all trials and tribulations, whether apparent or hidden, especially in our country Singapore and all the lands of the world, O Lord of the worlds. O Allah, turn away from us the evil of climatic changes and bring us their good. We ask that they be changes of mercy, not punishment, O Most Merciful. Our Lord, who besides You can protect us from this world's heat? Who besides You can safeguard us from the Hellfire's heat? There is no refuge but You. You are our Protector and Master; upon You we rely, to You we repent, and to You is the final return. Our Lord, grant us good in this world, good in the Hereafter, and save us from the Fire.*

## Section 5 · Source & Notes

**Reading instruction:** *Recite this du'ā 3 times before Maghrib on 29th Zulhijjah. The threefold repetition is a customary practice in many Muslim communities, especially in Southeast Asia.*

**Tradition source:** This du'ā is a compiled supplication commonly recited in Singaporean and Malaysian mosques during the end of the Islamic year. It is not found as a single continuous text in the canonical hadith collections, but it consists of several well‑known Qur'ānic phrases (such as *rabbanā ātinā fī d-dunyā...* — Qur'ān 2:201) and traditional invocations gathered from various scholarly sources. The invocation for climate mercy and for Gaza/Palestine reflects contemporary community concerns and is a pious addition not part of the classical text. Its overall structure follows the local *wirid* tradition. No single authentic hadith prescribes this exact wording, but each component is rooted in established du'ā literature.

# Doa Awal Muharram · New Year Supplication
### Recited after Maghrib on the eve of 1st Muharram, welcoming the new Islamic year with faith and hope

> **How to use:** Recite this du'ā 3 times after Maghrib on the 29th of Zulhijjah (i.e., at the beginning of the night of 1st Muharram). It is a prayer for a year of peace, protection from evil, and closeness to Allah.

---

## Section 3 · Seeking Protection & Help Against the Self

Our Lord, we praise and thank You for blessings that are countless and beyond measure. All praise and thanks are Yours whenever You are pleased with us.*

## Section 4 · Supplication for Relief, Protection, and Palestine

**Instruction:** *Do'a memohon keringanan dari perubahan iklim, perlindungan di hari kiamat, dan pertolongan untuk Palestina* — Supplication for relief from climate changes, protection on the Day of Judgment, and help for Palestine.

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Section 6 · Source & Notes

**Reading instruction:** *Recite this du'ā 3 times after Maghrib on the eve of 1st Muharram. The triple repetition follows the local custom of marking the new year with earnest supplication.*

**Tradition source:** This supplication is a compiled form widely used in Singaporean and Malaysian mosque circles during the arrival of Muharram. It draws upon classical invocations (e.g., *Allāhumma ahillahu ʿalaynā...* which appears in various hadith‑related literature about sighting the new moon) and adds contemporary concerns such as climate relief and the plight of Palestinians. The core of the du'ā is rooted in the broader tradition of seeking protection at the turn of the year, though the exact wording is not traceable to a single authentic hadith. It is best understood as a pious *wird* (litany) approved by local religious authorities for communal recitation.
