# Doa untuk Hamil
### Prayers for Pregnancy — Word-by-Word Guide

> **How to use:** A traditional compilation of supplications recited by Muslim women during pregnancy. Includes a daily du'ā after every prayer and a du'ā for safe delivery to be recited from the seventh month onward.

---

## Section 1 · Du'ā After Every Prayer

**Instruction:** *Setiap lepas sholat sambil memegang pusar baca do'a ini.* — After every obligatory prayer, while gently touching the navel, recite this du'ā.

> **Full meaning:** *O Allah, if You have created a being in this womb of mine, then form it as a male, and shape it in a beautiful, complete form. Make him a righteous man — knowledgeable, wise, obedient to Allah, His Messenger ﷺ, and his parents. Make him among those granted lawful, pure, blessed, abundant provision. Protect him from the two disgraces — poverty and debt. Keep him safe from the calamities of this world and the next, and from their trials. O Allah, do not make him among the people of harm, and guide us to the straight path. Āmīn, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful. All praise is for Allah, Lord of the worlds. My Lord, do not leave me alone, for You are the best of inheritors.*

## Section 2 · Du'ā for Safe Delivery

**Instruction:** *Dari bulan ketujuh sampai sembilan, baca Surat al-Fātiḥah, lalu shalawat 7 kali, lalu Surat At-Tāriq, lalu do'a ini sambil memegang dari punggung sampai perut, 7 kali.* — From the 7th to the 9th month: recite Sūrah al-Fātiḥah, then ṣalawāt upon the Prophet ﷺ seven times, then Sūrah At-Tāriq, then this du'ā while moving the hands from the back across the belly. Recite **seven times**.

> **Full meaning:** *O Allah, bring forth this child from my womb through my birth-canal at the time of delivery — safely and happily, in this world and the next. O Allah, accept my supplication, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, be gentle in easing every difficulty, and I ask You for ease and well-being in this world and the next.*

## Section 3 · Source & Notes

**Reading instruction:** *Lengkapi Surat At-Tāriq selepas do'a.* — After reciting this du'ā seven times, complete the rest of Sūrah At-Tāriq (verses 8–17).

**Tradition source:** This compilation is preserved in Malay/Indonesian *kitab kuning* and in Ḥaḍramī Yemeni midwifery manuals. It is a pious devotional composition and is **not directly traced to canonical hadith** (Bukhārī, Muslim, etc.). The closing line *"Rabbi lā tadharnī fardan wa anta khayru-l-wārithīn"* is the supplication of Prophet Zakariyyā (AS) — **Qur'ān 21:89**. Sūrah At-Tāriq referenced in the recitation order is **Qur'ān 86**. Asking specifically for a male child differs from the etiquette of Sūrah ash-Shūrā (42:49–50); reciters may choose to omit the word *dhakaran* and leave gender to Allah's wisdom.
